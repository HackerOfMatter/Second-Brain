**At a Glance:**  
Corporate and personal ethics refer to **moral responsibility in business conduct** — balancing profit with fairness, honesty, and social duty.

**In Depth:**

- **Corporate Ethics:** Standards guiding company decisions.
    
    - Includes [[Good Faith and Fair Dealing]], anti-bribery rules, and accountability to stakeholders.
        
    - Governed by corporate codes and legal compliance programs.
        
- **Personal Ethics:** Individual decision-making within corporate settings.
    
    - Responsibility to uphold ethical values, even under pressure.
        
    - Often tested in cases of [[Tortious Interference and Ethical Duties]].
        
- **Key Concepts:**
    
    - Conflicts of interest.
        
    - Whistleblowing and loyalty.
        
    - Fiduciary responsibility.
