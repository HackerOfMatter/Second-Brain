# Deontological
- Right thing to do no matter the cost
- Result > Reason/cost
# Natural Law
- Moral values found from an external source
	- Christian values come from the Bible
# Ulititarian
- Max happiness
- Minimum Pain
	- Cost/Benefit way of thinking
# Relativism
- Whatever culture says is okay is okay
- Cannot be proven right or wrong
# Egoism
- Motivated by self interest
	- Me
# Emotism
- Emotion based
- Whatever feels right, no objective truth
# Virtue Theory
- Whatever builds up ones own character
