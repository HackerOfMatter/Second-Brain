**At a Glance:**  
The **right action is the one that produces the greatest good for the greatest number**.

**In Depth:**

- **Philosophers:** Jeremy Bentham, John Stuart Mill.
    
- **Key Principle:** Utility = happiness or benefit to society.
    
- **Decision Rule:** Evaluate **outcomes**, not motives.
    
- **Example:**
    
    - A company lays off 5% of workers to save the business and protect 95% of jobs — judged ethical by utilitarian standards.
        

### ✅ Pros:

- Encourages **efficiency and overall welfare**.
    
- Pragmatic and outcome-focused.
    
- Useful in **policy and business ethics** (e.g., cost-benefit analysis).
    

### ⚠️ Cons:

- Can justify harming minorities for the “greater good.”
    
- Hard to predict all consequences.
    
- May conflict with rights or duties under [[Deontological Ethics (Duty-Based)]].
