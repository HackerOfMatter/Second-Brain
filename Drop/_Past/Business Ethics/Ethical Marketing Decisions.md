# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Ethical marketing balances persuasion with honesty, ensuring products and campaigns do not mislead or exploit consumers.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Key Principles:**  
  - Truthfulness and transparency in advertising.  
  - Respect for consumer autonomy — no manipulation or deception.  
  - Avoid discriminatory, fear-based, or exploitative tactics.  

- **Legal Roots:**  
  - Regulated by the FTC under the *Truth in Advertising* standards.
