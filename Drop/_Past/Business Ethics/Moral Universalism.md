**At a Glance:**  
Belief that **some moral principles are universally valid**, regardless of culture, time, or situation.

**In Depth:**

- **Principle:** Ethics are not purely relative — some acts are always wrong (e.g., torture, slavery).
    
- **Opposite of:** _Cultural Relativism_ (ethics vary by culture).
    
- **Use in Law:**
    
    - Supports international human rights laws and anti-corruption standards.
        
    - Influences global business ethics and cross-border corporate codes.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A multinational firm upholds anti-bribery rules in all countries, even where bribes are common practice.
