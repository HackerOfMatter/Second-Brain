# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Deontological ethics judges actions by moral duty, not consequences — doing the right thing simply because it is right.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Alaina Example:**  
  - A marketing director refuses to exaggerate claims about a drug’s benefits, despite pressure to meet sales goals.  
  - Her decision is guided by duty to truth and integrity, not profit.  
- **Principle:**  
  - Aligns with Immanuel Kant’s philosophy — actions must be universally moral, not situationally justified.
