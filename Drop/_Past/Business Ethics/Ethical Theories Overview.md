## 🧭 [[Ethical Theories Overview]]

**At a Glance:**  
Ethical theories are frameworks that help determine what is **right or wrong conduct**. In law and business, they guide decisions where **legal compliance** and **moral responsibility** may conflict.

**In Depth:**

- **Purpose:** Provide moral reasoning for choices affecting others.
    
- **Categories:**
    
    - [[Deontological Ethics (Duty-Based)]] → Focuses on moral duties regardless of outcomes.
        
    - [[Utilitarian Ethics (Consequentialism)]] → Focuses on outcomes and maximizing overall good.
        
    - [[Moral Universalism]] → Certain ethical standards apply to all people, everywhere.
        
- **Use in Law/Business:**
    
    - Helps evaluate actions beyond legality.
        
    - Influences [[Corporate and Personal Ethics]] and compliance codes.
        
    - Used in courts when assessing intent or fairness in decision-making.
