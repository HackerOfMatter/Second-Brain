# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Incrementalism explains how unethical behavior grows gradually through small, seemingly harmless steps.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Zenon Example:**  
  - A procurement officer accepts minor gifts “just this once.” Over time, larger unethical favors feel normal.  
- **Psychology:**  
  - People rationalize each small step, leading to ethical blindness.  
- **Prevention:**  
  - Clear ethical training and strong corporate culture to stop small compromises early.
