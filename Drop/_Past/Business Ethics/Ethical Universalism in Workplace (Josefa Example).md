# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Ethical universalism holds that moral principles apply equally across all cultures and business contexts.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Josefa Example:**  
  - A manager working abroad refuses to accept a “customary” gift that violates her company’s code of ethics.  
  - She upholds universal moral standards rather than cultural exceptions.  

- **Principle:**  
  - Ethical conduct should not depend on geography or tradition.  
  - Upholds consistency, integrity, and global corporate credibility.
