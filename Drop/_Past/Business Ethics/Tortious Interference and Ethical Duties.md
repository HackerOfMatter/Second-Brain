**At a Glance:**  
Occurs when one party **intentionally disrupts another’s contractual or business relationship** for unethical or unlawful gain.

**In Depth:**

- **Legal Definition:** Interference with existing or prospective contracts through deceit, coercion, or inducement.
    
- **Example:**
    
    - An employee convinces clients to leave their employer and follow them to a new company — both unethical and tortious.
        
- **Ethical Angle:**
    
    - Violates [[Good Faith and Fair Dealing]].
        
    - Contradicts [[Deontological Ethics (Duty-Based)]] (duty of loyalty) and [[Utilitarian Ethics (Consequentialism)]] (harms overall welfare).
        
- **Business Relevance:**
    
    - Common in cases of non-compete breaches and poaching clients.
