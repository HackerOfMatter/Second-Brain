# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Ethical contract performance rests on the deontological idea that parties have a duty to act honestly and fulfill promises regardless of outcome or profit.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Ethical Contracting** — forming and performing agreements guided by fairness, honesty, and moral duty.
        
    - **Deontological Ethics** — duty-based ethics asserting that right actions stem from adherence to moral rules, not results.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Ethical contracting reinforces trust and reputation.
        
    - The **duty to perform in good faith** mirrors Kantian duty — one should fulfill obligations because it is morally right.
        
    - Contracts should promote **mutual benefit**, **fair disclosure**, and **absence of coercion**.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A supplier honors price terms despite inflation, acting on duty rather than convenience.
        
    - A party refuses to exploit a loophole that undermines the spirit of the contract.
        
- **Ethical Framework in Law:**
    
    - Aligns with the legal doctrine of [[Good Faith and Fair Dealing]].
        
    - Reinforced through [[Corporate and Personal Ethics]] codes and fiduciary duties.
