**At a Glance:**  
Structured approaches to **evaluate ethical dilemmas** in business and legal decisions.

**In Depth:**

- **Purpose:** Ensure consistent, reasoned, and defensible ethical decisions.
    
- **Common Models:**
    
    1. **Utilitarian Approach:** Maximize total good.
        
    2. **Rights Approach:** Respect individual rights.
        
    3. **Fairness/Justice Approach:** Treat people equally unless there’s a justified reason.
        
    4. **Common Good Approach:** Promote societal well-being.
        
    5. **Virtue Approach:** Align with moral character and integrity.
        
- **Steps for Decision-Making:**
    
    1. Identify stakeholders.
        
    2. Gather facts.
        
    3. Evaluate with ethical theories (link: [[Ethical Theories Overview]]).
        
    4. Make and justify a choice transparently.
        
- **In Business Practice:**
    
    - Used in compliance, HR, and risk management decisions.
