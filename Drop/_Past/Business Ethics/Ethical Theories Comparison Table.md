# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A concise visual comparison of the major ethical frameworks guiding personal, legal, and business decisions.

# ⚖️ Ethical Frameworks Overview
| Theory | Core Principle | Moral Focus | Decision Approach | Key Thinker |
|---------|----------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Deontology** | Morality is based on duty and intent. | Rules & obligations | “Do what is right, regardless of the outcome.” | Immanuel Kant |
| **Utilitarianism** | Morality is based on maximizing happiness or utility. | Outcomes & consequences | “Choose what benefits the most people.” | John Stuart Mill |
| **Virtue Ethics** | Focuses on character and moral virtue. | Personal integrity | “Act as a virtuous person would act.” | Aristotle |
| **Moral Universalism** | Some moral standards are universal across cultures. | Fairness & equality | “Would this rule be fair if applied to all?” | John Rawls |
| **Ethical Egoism** | Actions are moral if they serve one’s rational self-interest. | Self-benefit | “What action best promotes my long-term interest?” | Ayn Rand |

# 💡 Notes
- **Deontology** → rule-based consistency.  
- **Utilitarianism** → practical and result-oriented.  
- **Virtue Ethics** → moral identity and intention.  
- **Universalism** → fairness applied globally.
