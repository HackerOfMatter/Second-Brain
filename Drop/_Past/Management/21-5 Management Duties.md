# 21-5 Management Duties 👔

**At a Glance:**  
Directors and officers owe **fiduciary duties** of care, loyalty, and good faith to the corporation and its shareholders.

---

## Fiduciary Duties
### 1. Duty of Care
- Act in good faith and with reasonable diligence (as a prudent person would).  
- Must make informed decisions.  
- **Business Judgment Rule:** Courts won’t second-guess decisions made in good faith with reasonable care.

### 2. Duty of Loyalty
- Put the corporation’s interest above personal gain.  
- Avoid conflicts of interest, self-dealing, or competing with the corporation.

### 3. Duty of Good Faith
- Act with honesty of purpose and fair dealing.  
- Overlaps with loyalty and care.

---

## Breach Consequences
- **Derivative suits** by shareholders on behalf of the corporation.  
- Courts may impose damages, disgorgement, or removal.

---

## Examples
- Director votes to award contract to company he owns → *breach of loyalty.*  
- CEO makes major deal without reviewing data → *breach of care.*

---

## Exam Tip
- Business Judgment Rule = *shield* for directors acting in good faith.  
- Loyalty breaches = *strictly scrutinized.*
