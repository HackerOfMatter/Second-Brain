# [[Classical Schools of Management]]
	Classical Schools of Management: Follow a set structure, prioritize efficiency and effectiveness.

# [[ Behavioral Manage Theory]]
	Behavioral Management Theory: Understand human behavior at work to motivate employees and improve job satisfaction.


# [[Quantitative School of Management]]
	Quantitative School of Management: Use data and numbers to make decisions, prioritize productivity and efficiency.


# [[Contigency School of Management]]

	Contingency School of Management: Emphasize adaptability and flexibility, recognize the importance of situational factors.

# [[Quality School of Management]]
Quality School of Management: Focus on ongoing improvement, emphasize continuous learning and innovation.
