# 📚 Principles of Marketing — October 27, 2025

## 🧩 Topic
**Today’s Focus:**  
Defining what distribution channels are, understanding why they are needed, identifying and comparing the major types of distribution channels, and recognizing the design considerations and management challenges involved in creating an effective distribution system.

> Distribution channels are the paths or intermediaries that move goods and services from the producer to the final consumer, ensuring that value is delivered efficiently and conveniently.

## 🧠 Case Study / Example
**Case Name:** Coca-Cola’s Global Distribution Network  
**Key Problem:** Managing a consistent brand and product availability worldwide.  
**Applied Strategy:** Coca-Cola uses local bottlers and distributors to adapt to market conditions while maintaining global quality standards.  
**Results / Takeaways:** Strategic partnerships and clear contracts reduce channel conflict and improve responsiveness to local markets.

## 💬 Instructor Highlights
- “Distribution is not just about moving products — it’s about moving *value*.”  
- “Every intermediary must add something meaningful — otherwise, it’s a cost, not a contribution.”  
- “Design channels backward: start with your customer and design the path that gets value to them efficiently.”
