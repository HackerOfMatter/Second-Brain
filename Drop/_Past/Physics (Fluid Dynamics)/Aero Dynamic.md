## 🔁 V. BIG PICTURE TAKEAWAYS

1. **Air is always trying to reach balance (equilibrium)**.
    
    - Any disturbance (wind, opening doors) creates imbalances.
        
2. **Fast-moving air creates low pressure.**
    
    - This is key to lift, wind tunnels, slipstreaming, and air movement.
        
3. **Air flows from high to low pressure**.
    
    - That's why you get breezes, suction, and door slams.
        
4. **Structures can trap or resist pressure changes.**
    
    - Tents, homes, cars, and even your lungs!
