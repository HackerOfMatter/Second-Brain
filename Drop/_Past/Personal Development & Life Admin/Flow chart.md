───────────────────────────────
        LABOR LAW
───────────────────────────────
Union Formation → Employee Rights
- Authorization cards → ≥30% triggers NLRB election
- Employer cannot:
  - Interfere / coerce
  - Reward non-union selectively
- NLRA Violation → illegal benefits to non-union

───────────────────────────────
       SECURITIES & INSIDER
───────────────────────────────
SEC Registration → Investment
- Material Nonpublic Info → Insider trading
- Tippee liable if trades using tip
- IPO → Registration Statement, SEC review
---
───────────────────────────────
         BANKRUPTCY
───────────────────────────────
Type → Who → Purpose
- Chapter 7 → Individuals/Businesses → Liquidation
- Chapter 11 → Businesses → Reorganization
- Chapter 12 → Farmers → Keep farm
- Chapter 13 → Individuals → Repayment plan
- Subchapter 5 → Small Business → Simplified reorg
Notes:
- Non-dischargeable: taxes, student loans, fraud, child support
- Trustee collects assets, divides to creditors
---
───────────────────────────────
    CONSUMER / CREDIT / INSURANCE
───────────────────────────────
Credit Cards:
- Timely notice → limit liability (CARD Act)
Insurance:
- Life → Term (temporary), Whole (perm + cash)
- Key Person → protects business
- Liability → property / injury
- Auto → collision / comprehensive
FTC → §5 prohibits unfair/deceptive acts
False Advertising → illegal if unsupported

---
───────────────────────────────
       ENVIRONMENTAL LAW
───────────────────────────────
Key Acts:
- CWA → Permits for point-source discharge
- RCRA → Hazardous/nonhazardous waste
- CERCLA → Cleanup liability
- ESA → Protect endangered species
- TSCA → Chemicals regulation
Impact Statement → Required for federal actions
Stationary Sources → Factories / power plants

---
───────────────────────────────
      INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
───────────────────────────────
Patent → Protect invention, synthetic gene patentable
Trademark → Protect brand, priority = first use/registration
Copyright → Protect creative works, fair use applies
Infringement → Likelihood of confusion

---
───────────────────────────────
      PROPERTY / BAILMENTS
───────────────────────────────
Bailment Care:
- Sole benefit of bailor → minimal care
- Sole benefit of bailee → high care
- Mutual benefit → ordinary care
Landlord Duties:
- Deliver possession, habitability
Tenant Duties:
- Pay rent, preserve property
Gifts:
- Must intend + deliver
- Conditional gifts → enforceable only if condition met

---
───────────────────────────────
      CONTRACTS / WARRANTIES
───────────────────────────────
- Express → explicit promise
- Implied → ordinary use, merchantability
- Misrepresentation → false advertising → liability
Quick Mnemonic: “EI + M” → Express, Implied, Misrepresentation
