Also a false-positive class: "do 1/2 the chapter" was parsing as 2 January and "read pages 3/4" as 4 March. A slash date now only counts when something marks it as one — by 9/8, a four-digit year, or being the whole capture. Both of those now yield no date, which is correct.

New phrases that work: eow, eom, end of the month, bare eod, this/next weekend, 8-25, the 30th, 2 weeks from now.

The specific format you asked about: due: 2026-09-08 or deadline = 9/8 is read literally and never re-interpreted. title = date shortcuts
