Honey Garlic Chicken Tenders (Crispy or Sticky-Glazed)

  

  

You can make these two ways:

  

- Lightly pan-seared for a healthier, saucy option.
- Crispy fried with a sticky honey garlic glaze (more indulgent).

  

  

  

  

  

🔪 

Ingredients

  

  

For the Chicken Tenders:

  

- 1.5 lbs chicken breast tenders
- Salt and pepper
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp cornstarch or arrowroot starch (for light crispness)
- 2 tbsp oil (avocado, canola, or light olive oil)

  

  

Optional Crispy Version (breading):

  

- 1 egg, beaten
- ½ cup coconut flour or panko (or combo)
- 2 tbsp cornstarch or arrowroot

  

  

For the Honey Garlic Sauce:

  

- ¼ cup honey
- 3–4 cloves garlic, finely minced (or grated)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional but adds depth)
- ¼ tsp chili flakes (optional, for heat)
- 1 tsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp water (for thickening slurry)

  

  

  

  

  

👨‍🍳 

Instructions

  

  

  

🔥 Step 1: Season and Prep Chicken

  

  

- Pat tenders dry and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
- Dust with cornstarch to help with browning and a slight crisp.

  

  

Optional: For a crispy version, dip in egg → dredge in coconut flour/starch → fry in oil until golden.

  

  

  

  

🍳 Step 2: Cook Chicken

  

  

- Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
- Add chicken in batches; don’t overcrowd.
- Cook ~3–4 minutes per side, until golden and internal temp is 165°F (74°C).
- Set aside on a plate.

  

  

  

  

  

🍯 Step 3: Make the Honey Garlic Sauce

  

  

- Lower heat to medium. In the same pan, add a tiny splash of oil if dry.
- Sauté garlic for 30 seconds—don’t burn it.
- Add honey, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and chili flakes (if using).
- Bring to a simmer.
- Mix cornstarch + water and stir in to thicken. Simmer for ~1 minute.

  

  

  

  

  

🔁 Step 4: Glaze the Chicken

  

  

- Return cooked chicken to the pan.
- Toss to coat evenly in the sauce.
- Simmer for 1–2 minutes, until everything is glossy and sticky.

  

  

  

  

  

🌿 

Garnish Ideas

  

  

- Sesame seeds
- Sliced green onions
- Crushed red pepper
- Lime wedges (for contrast)

  

  

  

  

  

🍚 

Serving Suggestions

  

  

- Over jasmine or coconut rice
- With steamed broccoli or snap peas
- In a lettuce wrap with cucumbers and carrots
- Over noodles with a squeeze of lime

  

  

  

  

  

🧠 

Chef’s Tips

  

  

- For deeper flavor: Add a splash of chicken stock or butter to the sauce.
- Use a microplane for garlic if you want a more even, mellow flavor.
- No honey? Try maple syrup or agave—still delicious.
