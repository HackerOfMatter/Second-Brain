# Monopoly - Profit Maximization & Inefficiency

#LumenModule9 #Monopoly #ProfitMax

In Lumen Microeconomics Module 9, a **monopoly** is a market structure with a single firm producing a product with no close substitutes.

### 📐 80/20 Core Rule
1. **Profit Maximization Condition:**
   $$\text{MR} = \text{MC}$$
2. **Downward-Sloping Marginal Revenue:**
   Because a monopolist faces the entire market demand curve, to sell additional units it must lower the price on *all* units sold. Thus, **$\text{MR} < P$** for any $Q > 1$.
3. **Determining Price and Profit:**
   * Find $Q^*$ where $\text{MR} = \text{MC}$.
   * Trace vertically up to the **Demand Curve ($D$)** to find price $P^*$.
   * $\text{Profit} = (P^* - \text{ATC}) \times Q^*$.

### 🚨 Economic Inefficiency
* **Allocative Inefficiency:** Monopolies produce where $P > \text{MC}$, producing less output and charging a higher price than a competitive market.
* **Deadweight Loss:** The net loss of total surplus (consumer + producer surplus) resulting from output restricted below the socially optimal quantity ($P = \text{MC}$).
