# Monopolistic Competition - Long Run Equilibrium & Advertising

#LumenModule10 #MonopolisticCompetition #ProductDifferentiation

Module 10 covers markets featuring many sellers with **differentiated products**.

### 🔄 Short-Run vs. Long-Run Dynamics
1. **Short Run:** Firms set $\text{MR} = \text{MC}$ to maximize profit. Can earn positive economic profit ($P > \text{ATC}$) or losses ($P < \text{ATC}$).
2. **Long-Run Adjustment:** 
   * **Free Entry/Exit:** Positive profits attract new firms, shifting the individual firm’s demand curve to the **left**.
   * **Long-Run Equilibrium:** $P = \text{ATC}$ at the profit-maximizing output $Q^*$. Economic profit is zero.
   * **Excess Capacity:** Firms produce at an output level below the minimum of the Average Total Cost curve ($P > \text{min ATC}$).

### 📣 Role of Advertising
* **Goal:** Increase demand (shift $D$ right) or make demand less elastic (steeper $D$) by convincing buyers of differentiation.
* **Tradeoff:** Increases costs ($\text{ATC}$ shifts up), but can provide information or lower search costs.
