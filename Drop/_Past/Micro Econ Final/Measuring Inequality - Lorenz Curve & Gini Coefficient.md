# Measuring Inequality - Lorenz Curve & Gini Coefficient

#LumenModule13 #IncomeInequality #LorenzCurve

Module 13 focuses on quantifying how income is distributed across a society.

### 📈 The Lorenz Curve
A graphical representation showing the cumulative percentage of population on the horizontal axis against the cumulative percentage of income received on the vertical axis.
* **45-Degree Line:** Represents perfect income equality.
* **Curved Line:** The further the Lorenz Curve bows away from the 45-degree line, the higher the inequality.

### 🔢 The Gini Coefficient
Calculated from the Lorenz curve area:
$$\text{Gini} = \frac{\text{Area between Line of Equality and Lorenz Curve}}{\text{Total Area under Line of Equality}}$$

* **Gini = 0:** Perfect Equality.
* **Gini = 1:** Absolute Inequality (1 person gets all income).
