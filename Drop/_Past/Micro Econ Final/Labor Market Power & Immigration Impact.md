# Labor Market Power & Immigration Impact

#LumenModule12 #LaborEcon #Monopsony

### 🏢 Imperfect Labor Markets
* **Monopsony (Employer Power):** A single buyer of labor (e.g., isolated town employer). 
  * Faces an upward-sloping labor supply curve.
  * Sets Marginal Cost of Labor ($\text{MCL}$) above the wage.
  * Employs **fewer workers** and pays **lower wages** than a competitive labor market.
* **Unions (Employee Power):** Labor organization that bargains collectively over wages and conditions, shifting supply to raise wages.

### 🌐 Economic Impact of Immigration
* **Supply Effect:** Shifts the labor supply curve to the right, slightly putting downward pressure on wages for close-substitute native workers.
* **Demand Effect:** Immigrants consume goods and services, shifting final product demand and labor demand curves to the right.
* **Complementarity:** Immigrants often complement native workers with different skill sets, increasing overall economic productivity.
