# Oligopoly, Collusion, & Prisoner's Dilemma

#LumenModule10 #Oligopoly #GameTheory

An **Oligopoly** consists of a small number of large firms dominating an industry, characterized by **mutual interdependence**.

### ⚔️ Collusion vs. Competition
* **Collusion:** Firms act together like a single monopoly to hold output down and keep prices high (forming a **Cartel**).
* **Breakdown:** Each individual firm has a private incentive to cheat on the agreement by producing slightly more to capture extra profit.

### ♟️ The Prisoner's Dilemma
A foundational Game Theory matrix demonstrating why cooperation is difficult to maintain even when it is mutually beneficial.

| | Firm B: Cooperate (Hold Output) | Firm B: Cheat (High Output) |
|---|---|---|
| **Firm A: Cooperate** | Monopoly Profits split (\$100, \$100) | Low Profit for A, High for B (\$20, \$150) |
| **Firm A: Cheat** | High Profit for A, Low for B (\$150, \$20) | Lower Competitive Profits (\$50, \$50) |

* **Dominant Strategy:** A strategy that is best for a player regardless of what the opponent plays.
* **Nash Equilibrium:** (Cheat, Cheat) $\rightarrow$ Outcome (\$50, \$50). Neither player has an incentive to unilaterally deviate.
