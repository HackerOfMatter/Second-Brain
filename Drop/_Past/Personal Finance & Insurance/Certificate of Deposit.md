# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A certificate of deposit (CD) is a bank’s written acknowledgment of receiving money and a promise to repay it at a future date with interest.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Certificate of Deposit** — a promissory note issued by a bank to a depositor.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Time deposit — not payable on demand.
        
    - Includes fixed maturity date and interest rate.
        
    - Negotiable if it meets Article 3 requirements.
