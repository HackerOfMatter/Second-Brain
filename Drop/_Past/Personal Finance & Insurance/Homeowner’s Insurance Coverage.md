### **Key Takeaways**

- **Homeowner’s insurance** protects against **financial loss** due to damage, theft, or liability on one’s property.
    
- Coverage typically includes **five main categories**:
    
    1. Building and other structures
        
    2. Additional living expenses
        
    3. Personal property
        
    4. Personal liability and related coverage
        
    5. Specialized coverage
        
- Policies protect not only the **main dwelling** but also **secondary structures and landscaping.**

| **Term**                             | **Definition**                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Homeowner’s Insurance**            | A policy providing financial protection against property damage, theft, and liability. |
| **Additional Living Expenses (ALE)** | Reimbursement for costs when a home becomes temporarily uninhabitable.                 |
| **Personal Property**                | Movable belongings inside the home covered under the policy.                           |
| **Liability Coverage**               | Protection against legal responsibility for injuries or property damage.               |
| **Specialized Coverage**             | Optional extensions for excluded risks (e.g., floods, earthquakes).                    |
| **Detached Structures**              | Secondary buildings (garage, shed) included in building coverage.                      |
For people who rent, home insurance coverage includes personal property protection, additional living expenses coverage, and personal liability and related coverage. Renter’s insurance does not provide coverage on the building or other structures.
