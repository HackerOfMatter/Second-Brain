Agency and Governance  CH 1.5

- Explain the agency problem: The possibility of conflict of interest between the owners and management of a firm. ([CH 1.5, Page 20](https://prod.reader-ui.prod.mheducation.com/epub/sn_c922/data-uuid-ef1a26db4f8d4f2bbec0ca41b7d835e4))
    
- How conflicts arise between managers and shareholders: Management and stockholder interests might differ. For example, the owners of a firm may wish to take a certain investment, but management may not due to risks. If management does not take the investment, stockholders may lose out on a valuable opportunity - this is called agency cost. ([CH 1.5, pg 20](https://prod.reader-ui.prod.mheducation.com/epub/sn_c922/data-uuid-ef1a26db4f8d4f2bbec0ca41b7d835e4))
