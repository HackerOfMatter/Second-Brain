## 🧩 Step-by-Step Framework (Steve’s Way)

### 1. 🧮 Identify Bond Details

Write down the core terms:

- **Face value (par)**
    
- **Stated (contract) rate**
    
- **Market (effective) rate**
    
- **Term (in years)**
    
- **Payment frequency** (e.g., semiannual)
    
- **Issue date and maturity date**
    
- **Issue price (at par, discount, or premium?)**
    

📌 This lets you determine how cash and interest behave.

---

### 2. 💸 Compute Cash Interest Payment

```markdown
Cash Interest = Face Value × Stated Rate × Time
```

- Time = 6/12 for semiannual
    
- This is the actual **cash paid** every period
    

✅ Used in both effective and straight-line methods.

---

### 3. 🧾 Record Initial Issuance Entry

Depending on whether bond is sold:

- **At par**:
    
    ```text
    Dr Cash  
    Cr Bonds Payable  
    ```
    
- **At discount**:
    
    ```text
    Dr Cash  
    Dr Discount on Bonds Payable  
    Cr Bonds Payable  
    ```
    
- **At premium**:
    
    ```text
    Dr Cash  
    Cr Premium on Bonds Payable  
    Cr Bonds Payable  
    ```
    

✅ Steve says: Think in journal entries to anchor reality.

---

### 4. 🧮 Calculate Amortization (Pick Method)

Choose:

- 🎯 **Straight-Line** (simpler):
    
    ```text
    Amortization = Total Premium or Discount ÷ # of periods
    ```
    
- 📉 **Effective-Interest** (accurate):
    
    1. Interest Expense = Carrying Value × Market Rate × Time
        
    2. Amortization = Interest Expense – Cash Interest
        
    3. Update carrying value
        

🔁 Repeat per period

---

### 5. 📘 Record Periodic Interest Entry

- **Discount** (expense > cash paid):
    
    ```text
    Dr Interest Expense  
    Cr Discount on Bonds Payable  
    Cr Cash  
    ```
    
- **Premium** (expense < cash paid):
    
    ```text
    Dr Interest Expense  
    Dr Premium on Bonds Payable  
    Cr Cash  
    ```
    

---

### 6. 💵 Record Retirement or Maturity

If bond matures:

```text
Dr Bonds Payable  
Cr Cash  
```

If retired early, compare book value to price paid — record gain/loss.

---

## 🧠 Mental Checklist for Any Bond Problem:

|✅|Question|
|---|---|
||Is the bond at par, discount, or premium?|
||What method am I using (straight-line or effective)?|
||What is the cash interest each period?|
||What is the carrying value over time?|
||What entries need to be recorded each period?|
