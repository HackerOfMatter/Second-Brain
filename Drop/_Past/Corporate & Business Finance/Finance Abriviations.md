## 💰 General Finance Symbols

|Symbol|Meaning|Notes / Units|
|---|---|---|
|**PV**|Present Value|Value today of future cash flows|
|**FV**|Future Value|Value at some time in the future|
|**r**|Interest rate / discount rate / required return|Annual rate unless stated otherwise|
|**t**|Time (number of periods)|Years, months, etc.|
|**C**|Cash flow (constant)|Used for annuities or coupons|
|**CFₜ**|Cash flow in period _t_|Subscript indicates year or period|
|**Σ**|“Sum of”|Add across all relevant periods|

## 📈 Stock Valuation (Dividends & Returns)

| Symbol       | Meaning                             | Notes                           |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **P₀**       | Current stock price (today’s value) | Intrinsic or market value       |
| **D₀**       | Most recent dividend just paid      | “Current” dividend              |
| **D₁**       | Next expected dividend              | Used in DDM formulas            |
| **g**        | Constant growth rate of dividends   | Must be less than r             |
| **r**        | Required rate of return on equity   | Also called _kₑ_ or _Re_        |
| **r_f**      | Risk-free rate                      | Typically U.S. T-bill rate      |
| **E[Rₘ]**    | Expected market return              | Used in CAPM                    |
| **β (beta)** | Measure of systematic risk          | Sensitivity to market movements |
| **MRP**      | Market Risk Premium = (E[Rₘ] – r_f) | Used in CAPM                    |
| **E[Rᵢ]**    | Expected return on stock _i_        | From CAPM: r = r_f + β(MRP)     |

## 🏗️ Capital Budgeting

|Symbol|Meaning|Notes|
|---|---|---|

|**NPV**|Net Present Value|PV(inflows) – PV(outflows)|

|**PI**|Profitability Index|PV inflows / Initial cost|
|**OCF**|Operating Cash Flow|Cash from operations after tax|
|**NCS**|Net Capital Spending|Investment in fixed assets|
|**ΔNWC**|Change in Net Working Capital|Increase in CA – CL|
|**CFFA**|Cash Flow From Assets|OCF – NCS – ΔNWC|
|**Tc**|Corporate tax rate|Used in after-tax calculations|

## 🧠 Quick Mnemonics

- **D₁/P₀ + g = r** → Dividend model for _required return_.
    
- **C/r = PV** → Perpetuity value.
    
- **PV → FV** multiply by (1 + r)^t.
    
- **FV → PV** divide by (1 + r)^t.
    
- **NPV = inflows – outflows** → Positive → accept project.
    
- **WACC = weighted Re + Rd(1–T)** → Firm’s overall cost of funds.
