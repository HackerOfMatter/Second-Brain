# 3 General types of trades
**closed-end fund A fund** in which shares are issued by an investment company o==nly when the fund is organized.==
With closed-end funds no additional shares are issued by the company. Shares are purchased on the stock exchange

**exchange-traded fund (ETF**) A fund that invests in the stocks or other securities contained in a specific stock or securities ==index==.

**open-end fund** A mutual fund in which shares are issued and redeemed by the investment company at the request of investors.

The net asset value is calculated by dividing the current ==market== value of the fund's portfolio (reduced by liabilities) by the number of outstanding shares.

### load fund

A mutual fund (sometimes referred to as a_**ClassA**_fund) in which investors pay a commission every time they purchase shares.
