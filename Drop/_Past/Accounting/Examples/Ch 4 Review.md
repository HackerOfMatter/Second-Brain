# 📚 Accounting Skills Roadmap – What to Learn

## 🧾 1. Understand the Accounting Cycle
- Journal entries (regular and adjusting)
- Posting to trial balance
- Preparing adjusted trial balance
- Closing entries (revenues, expenses, dividends → Retained Earnings)
- Preparing financial statements

### 🔁 Practice:
- Manually create adjusting entries from trial balances
- Create and close temporary accounts
- Rebuild financial statements from scratch

---

## 📊 2. Classify and Analyze Accounts
### ✅ Temporary vs Permanent Accounts
- Temporary: Revenues, Expenses, Dividends (closed)
- Permanent: Assets, Liabilities, Equity (remain open)

### 💡 Know Normal Balances:
- Assets, Expenses, Dividends → Debit
- Liabilities, Revenues, Equity → Credit

---

## 🧮 3. Build and Interpret Financial Statements

### 📘 Multiple-Step Income Statement
- Break down into:
  - Net Sales = Sales – Discounts – Returns
  - Gross Profit = Net Sales – COGS
  - Operating Expenses: Split into Selling vs G&A
  - Net Income = Gross Profit – Expenses

### 📘 Single-Step Income Statement
- All Revenues – All Expenses = Net Income
- Group expenses into:
  - COGS
  - Selling Expenses
  - General & Administrative Expenses

---

## 🧾 4. Adjusting Entries (AJE) Essentials
### Common Adjustments:
- Supplies Used = Beginning – Ending
- Depreciation = Calculated annual expense
- Insurance = Expired portion → Insurance Expense
- Shrinkage = Inventory difference → COGS increase

### 🧠 Skill:
- Identify the adjusting entry from additional info
- Know which asset/expense accounts are affected

---

## 📈 5. Financial Ratios – Meaning & Calculation

### ✅ Current Ratio
- Formula: `Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities`
- Measures overall short-term liquidity

### ✅ Acid-Test (Quick) Ratio
- Formula: `(Cash + A/R + Short-Term Investments) ÷ Current Liabilities`
- Stricter test of liquidity (excludes inventory/supplies)

### ✅ Gross Margin Ratio
- Formula: `(Net Sales – COGS) ÷ Net Sales`
- Shows how much profit is left after covering inventory costs

---

## 🔎 6. Categorizing Expenses

### 🛍️ Selling Expenses:
- Sales Salaries
- Advertising
- Store Supplies
- Rent – Selling Space
- Depreciation – Store Equipment

### 🧾 G&A Expenses:
- Office Salaries
- Rent – Office
- Insurance
- Utilities (admin)
