### 🟦 **1. Cash Flows from Operating Activities (Indirect Method)**

Start with **net income**, then adjust for:

- **Non-cash expenses** (like depreciation)
    
- **Gains/losses on asset sales**
    
- **Changes in current assets and liabilities**
    

#### ➕ Net Income:

- Net income: **$114,975**
    

#### ➕ Adjustments for Non-Cash Items:

- Depreciation expense: **+ $20,750**
    
- **Loss** on sale of equipment: **+ $5,125** (added back because it reduced net income)
    

#### 🔁 Changes in Current Operating Assets and Liabilities:

|Account|Change|Cash Flow Impact|
|---|---|---|
|Accounts Receivable|Increase of $15,185 (65,810 – 50,625)|**− $15,185**|
|Inventory|Increase of $23,856 (275,656 – 251,800)|**− $23,856**|
|Prepaid Expenses|Decrease of $625 (1,875 – 1,250)|**+ $625**|
|Accounts Payable|Decrease of $61,534 (53,141 – 114,675)|**− $61,534**|

#### ✅ Net Cash from Operating Activities:

=114,975+20,750+5,125−15,185−23,856+625−61,534=$40,900= 114,975 + 20,750 + 5,125 - 15,185 - 23,856 + 625 - 61,534 = \boxed{\$40,900}=114,975+20,750+5,125−15,185−23,856+625−61,534=$40,900​

### 🟨 **2. Cash Flows from Investing Activities**

Look at **equipment** transactions and gains/losses:

#### Equipment Sale:

- Sold equipment:
    
    - Original cost = $46,875
        
    - Accumulated depreciation = $30,125
        
    - Book value = $16,750
        
    - Cash received = $11,625  
        🔻 Loss = $5,125
        
- Cash received = **+ $11,625**
    

#### Equipment Purchase:

- Purchased new equipment = **− $30,000 cash**
    
    - (Other $66,375 financed with notes payable)
        

#### ✅ Net Cash from Investing Activities:

=11,625−30,000=−$18,375= 11,625 - 30,000 = \boxed{-\$18,375}=11,625−30,000=−$18,375​

### 🧠 Learning Takeaways

- Depreciation and losses are **added back** to net income in the indirect method.
    
- Changes in working capital (A/R, inventory, etc.) are **adjustments** that reflect cash movement.
    
- Equipment purchases and sales are part of **investing activities**.
    
- Stock issuance, debt repayments, and dividends are part of **financing activities**.
