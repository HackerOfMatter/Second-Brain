🟨 Net Method  
────────────────────────

- You assume you will take the discount right away.
    
- Record the invoice at the discounted (net) amount.
    
- If you don’t take the discount, record it later as a loss or expense.
    

🧾 Example:  
Same $1,000 purchase, 2/10, net 30.

- Day 1: Record $980 (assumes 2% discount)
    
- If you miss discount → record $20 as “interest expense” or “discount lost”
    

👍 Encourages prompt payment and tracks missed discounts
