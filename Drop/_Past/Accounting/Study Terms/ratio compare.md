# 📊 Accounting Ratios

## 🧮 Current Ratio
- **Formula**: `Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities`
- **Measures**: Overall short-term liquidity
- **Healthy Range**: 1.5 – 2.0+

---

## 💧 Acid-Test Ratio (Quick Ratio)
- **Formula**: `(Cash + A/R + Short-Term Investments) ÷ Current Liabilities`
- **Excludes**: Inventory and Prepaid Expenses
- **Use When**: You need a stricter liquidity test
- **Healthy Range**: 1.0 or higher

---

## 💰 Gross Margin Ratio
- **Formula**: `(Net Sales – COGS) ÷ Net Sales`
- **Use**: To evaluate pricing strategy and production efficiency
- **Target**: Varies by industry (Retail: ~30-50%, Manufacturing: varies)

---

## 📌 Notes
- Current Ratio includes all current assets (even inventory & prepaid)
- Quick Ratio focuses only on the most liquid assets
- Gross Margin shows profit left **before** operating expenses
