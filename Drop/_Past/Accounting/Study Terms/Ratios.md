**Liquidity and Efficiency**

### Current Ratio

Ratio: **Current ratio**
Formula: `Current assets / Current liabilities`
Measure of: **Short-term debt-paying ability**

### Acid-Test Ratio

Ratio: **Acid-test ratio**
Formula: `(Cash + Short-term investments + Current receivables) / Current liabilities`
Measure of: **Immediate short-term debt-paying ability**

### Accounts Receivable Turnover

Ratio: **Accounts receivable turnover**
Formula: `Net sales / Average accounts receivable, net`
Measure of: **Efficiency of collection**

### Inventory Turnover

Ratio: **Inventory turnover**
Formula: `Cost of goods sold / Average inventory`
Measure of: **Efficiency of inventory management**

### Days' Sales Uncollected

Ratio: **Days' sales uncollected**
Formula: `(Accounts receivable, net / Net sales) × 365`
Measure of: **Liquidity of receivables**

### Days' Sales in Inventory

Ratio: **Days' sales in inventory**
Formula: `(Ending inventory / Cost of goods sold) × 365`
Measure of: **Liquidity of inventory**

### Total Asset Turnover

Ratio: **Total asset turnover**
Formula: `Net sales / Average total assets`
Measure of: **Efficiency of assets in producing sales**

**Solvency**

### Debt Ratio

Ratio: **Debt ratio**
Formula: `Total liabilities / Total assets`
Measure of: **Creditor financing and leverage**

### Equity Ratio

Ratio: **Equity ratio**
Formula: `Total equity / Total assets`
Measure of: **Owner financing**

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio

Ratio: **Debt-to-equity ratio**
Formula: `Total liabilities / Total equity`
Measure of: **Debt versus equity financing**

### Times Interest Earned

Ratio: **Times interest earned**
Formula: `(Income before interest expense and income tax expense / Interest expense)`
Measure of: **Protection in meeting interest payments**

**Profitability**

### Profit Margin Ratio

Ratio: **Profit margin ratio**
Formula: `Net income / Net sales`
Measure of: **Net income in each sales dollar**

### Gross Margin Ratio

Ratio: **Gross margin ratio**
Formula: `(Net sales - Cost of goods sold) / Net sales`
Measure of: **Gross margin in each sales dollar**

### Return on Total Assets

Ratio: **Return on total assets**
Formula: `Net income / Average total assets`
Measure of: **Overall profitability of assets**

### Return on Equity

Ratio: **Return on equity**
Formula: `Net income / Average total equity`
Measure of: **Profitability of owner investment**

### Basic Earnings per Share

Ratio: **Basic earnings per share**
Formula: `(Net income - Preferred dividends) / Weighted-average common shares outstanding`
Measure of: **Net income per common share**

**Market Prospects**

### Price-Earnings Ratio

Ratio: **Price-earnings ratio**
Formula: `Market price per common share / Earnings per share`
Measure of: **Market value relative to earnings**

### Dividend Yield

Ratio: **Dividend yield**
Formula: `(Annual cash dividends per share) / Market price per share`
Measure of: **Cash return per common share**
