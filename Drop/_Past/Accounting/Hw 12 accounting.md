Net income = $481,540

##### **2. Add Back Non-Cash Expenses**

Non-cash expenses reduce net income but **don’t use cash**.

- Depreciation: +$44,200
    
- Amortization (patents): +$4,200

+ 44,200 (Depreciation)
+  4,200 (Amortization)
= +48,400


Gains on fixed asset sales are **investing activities**, not operations.

- Gain on sale of equipment: −$6,200

– 6,200 (Gain on Sale of Equipment)


##### **4. Adjust for Working Capital Changes**

Changes in **current assets** and **current liabilities** affect cash:

|Change|Effect on Cash Flow|Rule|
|---|---|---|
|↑ Accounts Receivable $30,500|−30,500|Increase in asset = use of cash|
|↑ Inventory $25,000|−25,000|Increase in asset = use of cash|
|↓ Accounts Payable $12,500|−12,500|Decrease in liability = use of cash|
|↓ Salaries Payable $3,500|−3,500|Decrease in liability = use of cash|

Total WC Adjustment = –71,500




Net Income                          $481,540
+ Non-cash expenses                   48,400
– Non-operating gain                  (6,200)
– Working capital change             (71,500)
--------------------------------------------------
