## ⚖️ **Burden of Proof**

**Summary:**  
The **burden of proof** refers to which party must prove a claim and how convincingly it must be proven. It varies between criminal and civil cases.

**Key Points:**

- Comprises two parts:
    
    1. **Burden of production:** presenting sufficient evidence.
        
    2. **Burden of persuasion:** convincing the fact-finder.
        
- Prevents arbitrary decisions and ensures fairness in trials.
