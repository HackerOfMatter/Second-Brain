## ⚖️ **Administrative Law**

**Summary:**  
Administrative law governs the **creation, operation, and regulation of government agencies**. It defines **how agencies enforce statutory law** and **protects citizens** from arbitrary government action.

**Key Points:**

- Derived from **delegated legislative authority** under enabling statutes.
    
- Agencies can issue **rules (regulations)**, **orders**, and **adjudications**.
    
- Must comply with the **Administrative Procedure Act (APA)** (1946), which requires **notice-and-comment rulemaking** and allows **judicial review** of agency decisions.
    
- Serves as a bridge between broad statutes and practical enforcement.
    

**Example:**

- The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) creating emissions standards under the Clean Air Act.
    

**Compare:**

- [[Statutory Law]] provides the authority.
    
- [[Common Law / Case Law]] interprets the legality of agency actions.
