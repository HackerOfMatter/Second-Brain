# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Revisiting tortious interference highlights the ethical line between fair competition and deliberate sabotage of another’s business relationships.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Ethical Angle:**  
  - Beyond legality, intentional disruption of a competitor’s contracts violates **good faith competition** principles.  
  - Common in corporate raiding, poaching clients, or misrepresenting a rival’s capabilities.  

- **Corporate Responsibility:**  
  - Executives must ensure competitive practices align with ethical standards, not just legal thresholds.
