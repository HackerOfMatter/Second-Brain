# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Assault and battery are intentional torts — assault creates fear of harm; battery involves actual harmful or offensive contact.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Assault:**  
  - Intentional act causing reasonable apprehension of imminent harmful or offensive contact.  
  - No physical touch required.  

- **Battery:**  
  - Intentional, harmful, or offensive contact without consent.  
  - Includes indirect contact (e.g., hitting with an object).  

- **Defenses:**  
  - Consent (e.g., sports).  
  - Self-defense or defense of others.

Assault = *the threat*; Battery = *the hit*.  
Both protect the personal boundary between body and fear.
