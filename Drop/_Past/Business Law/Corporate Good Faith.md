# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Corporate good faith requires directors and officers to act honestly, loyally, and with a genuine belief that their decisions serve the company’s best interests.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Definition:**  
  - A standard of conduct that goes beyond mere compliance — it’s about integrity in decision-making.  

- **Application:**  
  - Tied to the fiduciary duty of loyalty.  
  - Breach occurs when decisions are made in bad faith or with intentional disregard for the company’s welfare (*In re Walt Disney Co. Derivative Litigation*).  

- **Examples:**  
  - Approving self-benefiting deals without disclosure.  
  - Concealing risk information from shareholders.
