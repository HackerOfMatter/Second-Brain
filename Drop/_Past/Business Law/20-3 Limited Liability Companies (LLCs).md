# 20-3 Limited Liability Companies (LLCs) ⚖️

**At a Glance:**  
Hybrid form combining limited liability of corporations with pass-through taxation of partnerships.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Articles of organization filed with state.  
- **Liability:** Members have limited liability.  
- **Taxation:** Pass-through unless electing corporate taxation.  
- **Control:** Managed by members or managers.  
- **Continuity:** Can continue after member departure (depends on operating agreement).

## Advantages
- Flexible structure  
- Limited liability  
- Pass-through taxation  

## Disadvantages
- More paperwork than sole proprietorship  
- Some states restrict formation
