# Powers of Congress

## 🔑 Article I, Section 1: Legislative Power

- **Power**: Makes laws to govern the United States
- **Key Terms**:
    - Enactment: The process of creating a law through the passage of a bill by both Houses of Congress and signing it into law by the President.
    - Appropriations: The process of allocating funds for specific purposes or programs.

---

## 🔒 Article I, Section 8: Taxing and Spending Power

- **Power**: Imposes taxes, regulates commerce, and spends government funds
- **Key Terms**:
    - Revenue Act: A law that sets the tax rates and procedures for collecting taxes.
    - Appropriations Bill: A bill that allocates funds for specific purposes or programs.

---

## 🔓 Article I, Section 9: Power to Regulate Commerce

- **Power**: Regulates commerce between states, with foreign nations, and among Indian tribes
- **Key Terms**:
    - Interstate Commerce: Trade and communication between states.
    - Foreign Commerce: Trade and communication between the United States and other countries.
    - Indian Commerce: Trade and communication between the United States and Native American tribes.

---

## 🔒 Article I, Section 10: Power to Regulate State Governments

- **Power**: Prohibits states from engaging in certain activities or imposing taxes on interstate commerce
- **Key Terms**:
    - Interstate Taxation: The taxation of goods and services that cross state lines.
    - Intra-State Commerce: Trade and communication within a state.

---

## 🔓 Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: Power to Establish Courts

- **Power**: Establishes courts inferior to the Supreme Court, including bankruptcy courts and trial courts
- **Key Terms**:
    - Inferior Courts: Courts that are lower in authority than the Supreme Court.
    - Bankruptcy Courts: Specialized courts that handle bankruptcy cases.

---

## 🔒 Article I, Section 8, Clause 4: Power to Regulate Currency

- **Power**: Establishes a uniform currency and regulates its use
- **Key Terms**:
    - Uniform Currency: A single standard of value for all transactions.
    - Fiat Currency: A currency that has no intrinsic value but is valued based on government decree.

---

## 🔓 Article I, Section 8, Clause 5: Power to Regulate Weights and Measures

- **Power**: Establishes a system of weights and measures for the United States
- **Key Terms**:
    - Standard Weights and Measures: A set of standard units of measurement for length, weight, volume, etc.
    - Metric System: A system of measurement that uses the meter as the fundamental unit of length.

---

## 🔒 Article I, Section 8, Clause 6: Power to Regulate Intellectual Property

- **Power**: Grants patents and copyrights to inventors and authors
- **Key Terms**:
    - Patent: A government-issued monopoly on an invention.
    - Copyright: A legal right granted to the author of a creative work.

---

## 🔓 Article I, Section 9: Power to Regulate Public Lands

- **Power**: Manages public lands, including national parks and forests
- **Key Terms**:
    - Public Lands: Land owned by the federal government.
    - National Parks: Designated areas of natural beauty or historical significance.
