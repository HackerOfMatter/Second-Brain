# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Article 2 governs the sale of tangible, movable goods, providing standardized rules for contracts, performance, and remedies in commercial transactions.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Sale of Goods** — transfer of ownership of tangible, movable items for a price.
        
    - **UCC (Uniform Commercial Code)** — harmonized set of business laws adopted by U.S. states to standardize commercial transactions.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Applies only to **goods**, not services or real estate.
        
    - Designed to simplify and modernize commerce.
        
    - Allows **gap-fillers** (default terms) where contracts are silent — e.g., price, delivery, payment timing.
        
    - Modified by [[Merchant Definition]] and [[Good Faith under UCC]].
        
- **Scope & Examples:**
    
    - Retail sales, manufacturing deliveries, and wholesale transactions.
        
    - Does not apply to **service-only contracts** or **employment agreements** (see [[Predominant Purpose Test (Goods vs Services)]])
