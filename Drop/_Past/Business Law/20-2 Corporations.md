# 20-2 Corporations 🏢

**At a Glance:**  
A separate legal entity owned by shareholders, offering limited liability and perpetual existence.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Requires filing articles of incorporation with state.  
- **Liability:** Shareholders have limited liability.  
- **Taxation:** Double taxation (corporate profits + dividends).  
- **Control:** Managed by board of directors; owned by shareholders.  
- **Continuity:** Perpetual existence.

## Advantages
- Limited liability  
- Easier access to capital  
- Perpetual life  

## Disadvantages
- Double taxation  
- Formalities and regulations
