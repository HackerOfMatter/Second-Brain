# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Everyone has a duty to act with reasonable care to avoid foreseeable harm to others.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - Legal obligation to adhere to a standard of reasonable conduct to protect others from unreasonable risk.
        
- **Factors:**
    
    - Relationship between parties.
        
    - Foreseeability of harm.
        
    - Public policy considerations.
        
- **Special Duties:**
    
    - Professionals, property owners, and employers owe heightened care.
