# [[Legislative]] (Congress)
**Powers**:
- Makes laws, 
- approves presidential appointments, 
- power to declare war 
- approve treaties
**Election Term/Period**: 
- 2-year terms for Representatives (House of Representatives);
- 6-year terms for Senators (Senate); 
	- both are elected by the people


# [[Executive]]
**Powers**:
- Enforces laws, 
- commander-in-chief of the military, 
- negotiates treaties  
- executive agreements
**Election Term/Period**: 
- 4 years for the President and Vice President; 
- no term limits (re-elected indefinitely)


# [[Judicial]]
 ### **Powers**: 
- Interprets laws, 
- settles disputes between states or federal government, 
- tries impeachments
**Election Term/Period**:
- Life term for Supreme Court justices (appointed by the President); 
- 15-year staggered terms for Circuit Court judges
