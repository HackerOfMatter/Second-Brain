# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Definition(s):**  
- **Bilateral**  — Both parties
	  - Ill paint your house for $
	  - Agreement = word
- **unilateral**  — One party promise
	- Dog owner promises reward for finding dog
	- agreement = act

- **Executory**  — Still has to hold up their end of the deal
- **Executed**  — Parties have fulfilled their promises

- **Valid**  — Satisfies laws requirement
	- Can win law suit
- **Voidable**  — LAw permits party to terminated agreeement
	- Minor makes contract
	- Fraudulant
- **Void**  — Neither party can enforce
	- Illegal
- **Unenforcable**  — Valid contract but court cant enforce it
- **Quasi**  — One party gets all the benefits while other gets nothing
	- "as if"
