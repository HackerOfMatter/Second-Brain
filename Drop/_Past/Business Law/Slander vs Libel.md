# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Slander is spoken defamation; libel is written or broadcast defamation — the difference lies in permanence and proof.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Slander:**  
  - Transient, oral statements.  
  - Requires proof of actual damage unless *slander per se* (e.g., false statements about crime, disease, or morality).  

- **Libel:**  
  - Written or recorded defamation.  
  - Presumes damages due to wider, lasting harm.
