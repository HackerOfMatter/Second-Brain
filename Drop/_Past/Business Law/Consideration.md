## ⚖️ At a Glance
**Consideration** is the value each party gives up or promises to give up in exchange for the other’s promise.

## 📚 In Depth
- Can be **money, services, forbearance**, or promises.  
- Must be **mutual and legal**; past acts or preexisting duties don’t count.  
- Courts rarely evaluate the **adequacy** of consideration, only its existence.

**Example:** Paying $1 for a house is still valid if both agreed freely.
