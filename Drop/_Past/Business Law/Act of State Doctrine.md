## 🌍 **Act of State Doctrine**

**Summary:**  
A judicial principle where U.S. courts **refrain from judging the acts of foreign governments** within their own territory.

**Key Points:**

- Promotes **respect for sovereignty** and avoids diplomatic tension.
    
- Separate from **sovereign immunity**, which concerns lawsuits _against_ foreign states.
    

**Example:**

- _Banco Nacional de Cuba v. Sabbatino_ (1964): courts refused to question Cuba’s nationalization of property.
