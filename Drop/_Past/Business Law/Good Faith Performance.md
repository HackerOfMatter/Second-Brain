# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Every contract includes an implied duty that parties will act honestly and fairly in carrying out their contractual obligations.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Good Faith Performance** — the obligation to act honestly, reasonably, and not undermine the purpose of the contract.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Derived from **common law** and the **UCC (§1-304)**.
        
    - Requires parties to **avoid deceptive conduct**, **cooperate** to achieve mutual benefit, and **not sabotage performance**.
        
    - Applies during both performance and enforcement of contracts.
        
- **Examples:**
    
    - A seller intentionally delays delivery to trigger late fees → breach of good faith.
        
    - A landlord refusing reasonable repairs to force a tenant out → bad faith performance.
