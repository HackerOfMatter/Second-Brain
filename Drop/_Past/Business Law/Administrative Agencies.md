# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Administrative agencies implement, regulate, and enforce laws under authority delegated by Congress or state legislatures.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Functions:**  
  - Rulemaking (quasi-legislative).  
  - Adjudication (quasi-judicial).  
  - Enforcement of compliance.  
- **Examples:**  
  - EPA, SEC, FCC, OSHA.  
- **Accountability:**  
  - Actions subject to [[Judicial Review]] for fairness and legality.
