## 🏛️ **Executive Branch**

**Summary:**  
Headed by the President, this branch **enforces and administers the law** through federal departments and agencies.

**Key Powers:**

- Issues executive orders.
    
- Oversees [[Administrative Law]] enforcement.
    
- Serves as Commander-in-Chief.
    

**Checks:**

- Congress controls funding and can override vetoes.
    
- Courts can strike down unconstitutional actions.
    

**Example:**

- President Franklin D. Roosevelt’s Executive Order 9066 (internment camps) — later ruled unlawful.
