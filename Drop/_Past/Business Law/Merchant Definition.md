# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A merchant is a person who regularly deals in goods of the kind or has special knowledge of the goods or trade practices.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Merchant (UCC §2-104)** — one who regularly buys or sells goods of the kind involved or has specialized business knowledge.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Merchants are subject to **higher standards of good faith** and commercial reasonableness.
        
    - Merchant status affects contract formation, warranties, and risk allocation.
        
    - Implies professional behavior — not ordinary consumer expectations.
        
- **Examples:**
    
    - A car dealer = merchant for car sales.
        
    - A teacher selling a used laptop = not a merchant for electronics.
