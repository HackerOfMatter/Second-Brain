# 21-2 Incorporation Process 🏗️

**At a Glance:**  
The legal creation of a corporation under state law. Establishes it as a separate entity distinct from its owners.

---

## Formation Steps
1. **Select State of Incorporation**
   - Choose based on corporate laws (e.g., Delaware favored for flexibility and case law).  

2. **Prepare and File Articles of Incorporation**
   - Filed with the state’s Secretary of State.  
   - Must include:  
     - Corporate name (“Corp.”, “Inc.”, etc.)  
     - Registered agent and office  
     - Purpose (broad purposes allowed under MBCA)  
     - Authorized shares and par value  
     - Names of incorporators  

3. **Hold Organizational Meeting**
   - Initial directors adopt **bylaws**, elect officers, and authorize share issuance.

4. **Issue Stock Certificates**
   - Confirms ownership rights.  
   - Must comply with securities law.

5. **Obtain EIN and Licenses**
   - Federal Employer ID; permits for operations.

---

## 🧠 Legal Doctrine
- **De Jure Corporation:** Properly formed, fully protected.  
- **De Facto Corporation:** Good-faith attempt to incorporate but technical error → treated as valid entity in most states.  
- **Corporation by Estoppel:** Third parties who deal with entity as if it were a corp. can’t later deny it’s a corp.

---

## Common Exam Traps
- Filing errors can lead to personal liability if no de facto status.  
- Corporation by estoppel protects *both sides* from denying existence.
