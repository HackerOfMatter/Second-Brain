# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Legislative rules have the force of law; interpretive rules explain or clarify existing law.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Legislative Rules:**  
  - Issued under statutory authority.  
  - Require notice-and-comment procedures (APA compliance).  
  - Binding on the public.  

- **Interpretive Rules:**  
  - Clarify agency’s understanding of existing laws.  
  - No formal procedure required.  
  - Nonbinding guidance but influential in enforcement.
