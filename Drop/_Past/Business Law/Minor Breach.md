# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A minor breach is a small, nonessential failure that doesn’t destroy the contract but allows limited damages.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Minor (Partial) Breach** — slight deviation from contract terms that doesn’t undermine overall purpose.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - The nonbreaching party must still perform.
        
    - Can claim damages for the specific defect or delay.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A shipment arrives one day late but doesn’t impact business operations.
