# 21-4 Death of the Corporation ⚰️

**At a Glance:**  
Corporations can dissolve voluntarily, administratively, or judicially. The “death” ends legal existence and triggers asset distribution.

---

## Types of Dissolution
1. **Voluntary Dissolution**
   - By board resolution and shareholder approval.  
   - Must file **Articles of Dissolution**.  
   - Corporation winds up affairs: pay debts, distribute assets.

2. **Administrative Dissolution**
   - By state for failure to file reports or pay fees.  
   - May be reinstated after compliance.

3. **Judicial Dissolution**
   - Ordered by court for fraud, abuse, or shareholder deadlock.

---

## Winding Up
- Collect assets → Pay creditors → Distribute remainder to shareholders.  
- Officers act as trustees during winding up.

---

## Continuation
- **Survival Statute:** Allows suits for limited time after dissolution.

---

## Exam Tips
- Distinguish between “termination” (final) and “dissolution” (process).  
- Creditors always paid **before** shareholders.
