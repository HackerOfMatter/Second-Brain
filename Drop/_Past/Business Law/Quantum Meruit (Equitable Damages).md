# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Quantum meruit allows recovery of the reasonable value of services rendered when no valid contract exists or full performance wasn’t completed.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Quantum Meruit** — Latin for “as much as he deserves”; an equitable remedy that compensates for the value of work performed.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Used when:
        
        1. A contract is unenforceable or void.
            
        2. A party substantially performs but not fully.
            
        3. Services were provided with a reasonable expectation of payment.
            
    - Prevents **unjust enrichment** (one party unfairly benefits without paying).
        
    - Measured by **market value** or **reasonable worth** of the performance.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A builder completes 90% of a project before termination; court awards value of completed work minus costs.
