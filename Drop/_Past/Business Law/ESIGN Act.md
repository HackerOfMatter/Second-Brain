# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The ESIGN Act is a federal law that ensures electronic signatures and records are legally valid in interstate and foreign commerce.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Purpose:**  
  - To harmonize digital transaction laws across all states.  
  - Ensures e-signatures can’t be denied enforceability solely for being electronic.  

- **Key Requirements:**  
  - Intent to sign electronically.  
  - Consent to do business electronically.  
  - Ability to retain and reproduce records.  

- **Interaction with UETA:**  
  - UETA covers state transactions; ESIGN covers federal and interstate.  
  - Together, they create consistent nationwide electronic contracting.
