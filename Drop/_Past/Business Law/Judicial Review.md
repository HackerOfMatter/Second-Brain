# Judicial Review

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
**Judicial review** gives courts the power to determine whether laws or government actions violate the Constitution.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
- Origin: *Marbury v. Madison* (1803)
- Role: Ensures government acts within constitutional bounds.
- Levels:
  - Supreme Court = final authority on constitutional issues.
  - Lower courts apply and interpret the precedent.
- **Example:** Declaring a state law unconstitutional for violating free speech.

# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Judicial review gives courts the power to declare laws or executive actions unconstitutional.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Source:** *Marbury v. Madison* (1803).  
- **Principles:**  
  - Courts interpret and uphold the Constitution as the highest law.  
  - Ensures separation of powers and prevents abuse by other branches.  
  - Can invalidate state or federal acts that conflict with the Constitution.
