# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** When state and federal laws conflict, the federal law prevails under the Supremacy Clause.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Preemption Doctrine:**  
  - **Express Preemption:** Congress explicitly displaces state law.  
  - **Implied Preemption:** Federal regulation is so pervasive that no room remains for states.  
- **Concurrent Powers:**  
  - Both levels of government may legislate in some areas (e.g., taxes, environmental law).  
  - Conflict occurs when compliance with both is impossible.
