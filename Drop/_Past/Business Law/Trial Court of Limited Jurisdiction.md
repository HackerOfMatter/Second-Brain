# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Trial courts of limited jurisdiction handle specific case types such as small claims, probate, traffic, or family matters.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Examples:**  
  - Small claims courts.  
  - Municipal or traffic courts.  
  - Family or juvenile courts.  
- **Principle:**  
  - Authority restricted to particular subjects or dollar limits.  
  - Appeals generally go to a general or [[Appellate Court]].
