# 20-1 Sole Proprietorships 🧍‍♂️

**At a Glance:**  
Single-owner business with full control and unlimited personal liability.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** No formal paperwork; created automatically when business starts.  
- **Liability:** Owner is personally responsible for all debts and obligations.  
- **Taxation:** Pass-through (income reported on owner's personal tax return).  
- **Management:** Complete control by owner.  
- **Continuity:** Business ends when owner dies or sells.  

## Advantages
- Easy to start and manage  
- Owner keeps all profits  
- Fewer regulations  

## Disadvantages
- Unlimited liability  
- Limited access to capital  
- No continuity  

## Compare & Contrast
- Similar to: [[20-5 General Partnerships]] (shared control, shared liability)  
- Opposite of: [[20-2 Corporations]] (separate legal entity, limited liability)
