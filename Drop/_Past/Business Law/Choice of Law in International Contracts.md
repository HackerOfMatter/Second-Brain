# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A choice-of-law clause determines which nation’s laws govern an international business contract.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Purpose:**  
  - Provides predictability and minimizes legal uncertainty across borders.  

- **Principles:**  
  - Must have a substantial relationship to the chosen jurisdiction.  
  - Cannot violate fundamental public policy.  
  - May interact with treaties like the CISG (Contracts for International Sale of Goods).  

- **Example:**  
  - A U.S.-EU software contract may specify “governed by the laws of California.”
