# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The Supreme Court struck down the presidential line-item veto as unconstitutional because it violated separation of powers.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Case:** *Clinton v. City of New York* (1998).  
- **Holding:**  
  - Line-item veto allowed the president to cancel parts of spending bills after signing them — effectively rewriting laws.  
  - Court ruled this violated Article I (only Congress can legislate).
