# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Priority rules determine which creditor gets paid first when multiple parties have claims to the same collateral, especially in bankruptcy.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Priority** — order of rights among competing secured creditors.
        
    - **Bankruptcy Effect** — determines who recovers collateral value first under federal bankruptcy law.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - **First to perfect, first in right.**
        
    - **Purchase-Money Security Interests (PMSI)** may take priority over earlier filings.
        
    - **Bankruptcy** generally freezes actions but respects perfected security interests.
