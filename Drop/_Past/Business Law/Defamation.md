# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Defamation is a false, damaging statement about someone’s reputation communicated to others.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Elements:**  
  1. False and defamatory statement.  
  2. Publication to a third party.  
  3. Fault amounting to negligence or actual malice.  
  4. Damage to reputation.  

- **Defenses:**  
  - Truth.  
  - Opinion (not presented as fact).  
  - Privilege (absolute or qualified).
