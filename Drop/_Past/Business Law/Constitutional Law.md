# Constitutional Law

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
Constitutional law establishes the **structure, powers, and limits** of government and guarantees fundamental rights.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
- **Federal vs. State Constitutions:**  
  - Federal Constitution is supreme; state constitutions cannot conflict.
- **Separation of Powers:**  
  - Legislative, Executive, and Judicial branches.
- **Checks and Balances:**  
  - Prevents one branch from overreaching.
- **Judicial Review:**  
  - Established in *Marbury v. Madison* — courts can invalidate unconstitutional acts.
