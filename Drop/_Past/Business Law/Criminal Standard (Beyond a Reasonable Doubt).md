## ⚖️ **Criminal Standard (Beyond a Reasonable Doubt)**

**Summary:**  
In criminal cases, the prosecution must prove every element of the crime **beyond a reasonable doubt**, meaning there is **no reasonable uncertainty** about guilt.

**Key Points:**

- Highest legal standard due to the **potential loss of liberty or life**.
    
- Ensures the presumption of innocence remains strong.
    
- The defendant never has to prove innocence.
    

**Example:**

- In a murder trial, the jury must be morally certain of guilt based on the evidence.
