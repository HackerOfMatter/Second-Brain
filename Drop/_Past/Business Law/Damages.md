# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Damages in negligence aim to make the injured party “whole” — restoring them to the position before the harm occurred.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Types:**
    
    - **Compensatory:** medical bills, lost wages, pain and suffering.
        
    - **Punitive:** punish egregious behavior.
        
    - **Nominal:** token award recognizing rights violation.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Must be actual and proximate result of the breach.
        
    - Duty to mitigate damages applies.
