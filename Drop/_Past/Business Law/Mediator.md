# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A mediator is a neutral facilitator who helps disputing parties reach a voluntary, mutually acceptable agreement.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Role:**  
  - Guides communication, identifies interests, proposes compromises.  
  - Does not issue binding decisions.  

- **Advantages:**  
  - Preserves relationships.  
  - Confidential and flexible.  
  - Often faster and cheaper than arbitration or litigation.
