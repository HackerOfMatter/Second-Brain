🧠 Core Idea

  

  

⚡ In essence: Under strict liability, a party is responsible for harm caused by their actions or products regardless of intent or negligence.

  

  

🔍 Key Concepts

  

  

- Definition(s):  
    

- Strict Liability — legal responsibility for damages even without proof of fault or carelessness.

-   
    
- Principles:  
    

- Applied in inherently dangerous activities or defective product cases.
- Focus is on causation and risk, not conduct.
- Defenses like “reasonable care” usually do not apply.
