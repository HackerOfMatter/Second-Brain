# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The UETA validates electronic records, signatures, and contracts — giving them the same legal effect as paper documents.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Purpose:**  
  - To make electronic transactions legally equivalent to written ones.  
  - Encourages commerce by recognizing digital consent and records.  

- **Scope:**  
  - Applies to commercial, consumer, and governmental transactions.  
  - Adopted by most U.S. states (except Illinois and New York, which have similar laws).  

- **Principles:**  
  - Parties must agree to transact electronically.  
  - Electronic signatures show intent and consent.
