# 20-8 Franchises 🍔

**At a Glance:**  
Agreement allowing one business (franchisee) to use another’s trademark and model.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Contract between franchisor and franchisee.  
- **Liability:** Separate legal entities; governed by contract.  
- **Taxation:** Each pays own taxes.  
- **Control:** Franchisor provides standards; franchisee operates locally.  

## Advantages
- Brand recognition  
- Support and training  

## Disadvantages
- Limited independence  
- Fees and royalties
