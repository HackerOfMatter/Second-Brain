## ⚖️ **Line Item Veto (Unconstitutional)**

**Summary:**  
A **line-item veto** allows executives to remove specific spending provisions from a bill. The Supreme Court found it **unconstitutional** in _Clinton v. City of New York (1998)_.

**Reason:**

- Violates **Separation of Powers**.
    
- Only Congress has the power to modify legislation.
