# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Indorsement is the act of signing and transferring a negotiable instrument to another person, making them the new holder with rights to payment.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Indorsement** — a signature (usually on the back of the instrument) that transfers ownership or rights to enforce it.
        
- **Types of Indorsement:**
    
    - **Blank** — just a signature; instrument becomes payable to bearer.
        
    - **Special** — specifies a new payee (e.g., “Pay to Alex Smith”).
        
    - **Restrictive** — limits use (“For deposit only”).
        
    - **Qualified** — adds disclaimer (“Without recourse”).
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Transfers rights but also potential liability if dishonored.
        
    - A valid indorsement makes the recipient a **holder in due course** (protected from some defenses).
