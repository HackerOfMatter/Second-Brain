# Bankruptcy — HW & Quiz Cheat Sheet (lookup)

## Core definitions (one line each)
- Automatic stay — halts most collection at filing; exceptions exist (criminal, some family law).
- Discharge — legal release of personal liability for certain debts; exceptions apply.
- Estate — all legal/equitable interests at petition time (with exclusions).

## Red flags for avoidance
- **Preference**: transfer to creditor, antecedent debt, within 90 days (1 year for insiders), debtor insolvent, enables more than Ch7.
- **Fraudulent transfer**: actual intent OR received less than reasonably equivalent value + insolvency indicators.
