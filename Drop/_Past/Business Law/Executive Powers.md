# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The executive branch enforces laws, commands the military, and influences legislation — balanced by Congress and the courts.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Constitutional Basis:** Article II of the U.S. Constitution.  
- **Powers Include:**  
  - Appointing officials and judges.  
  - Issuing executive orders.  
  - Vetoing congressional acts.  
  - Conducting foreign policy.
