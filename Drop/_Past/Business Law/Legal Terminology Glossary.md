# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A quick-access glossary of essential legal terms used across contract, tort, and constitutional law.

# 📘 Key Terms
| Term | Definition |
|------|-------------|
| **Plaintiff** | Person bringing a lawsuit. |
| **Defendant** | Person or entity being sued. |
| **Tort** | Civil wrong causing harm or loss. |
| **Precedent** | A previous case serving as authority. |
| **Jurisdiction** | Court’s authority to hear a case. |
| **Stare Decisis** | Doctrine requiring courts to follow precedent. |
| **Subpoena** | Court order requiring testimony or evidence. |
| **Statute** | Law enacted by a legislature. |
| **Ordinance** | Local or municipal law. |
| **Summary Judgment** | Court decision without full trial when facts are undisputed. |
