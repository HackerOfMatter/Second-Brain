# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** The plaintiff must prove that the defendant’s breach actually and legally caused the injury.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Types:**
    
    - **Actual Cause (Cause-in-Fact):** “But-for” test — the harm wouldn’t occur but for the defendant’s act.
        
    - **Proximate Cause (Legal Cause):** Foreseeability — was the harm a natural result of the breach?
        
- **Case Example:**
    
    - _Palsgraf v. Long Island Railroad_ — established foreseeability in proximate cause.
