# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** An arbitration panel is a group of neutral arbitrators chosen to hear evidence and issue a binding decision outside court.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Composition:**  
  - Typically one or three arbitrators.  
  - Selected by parties or an arbitration institution (e.g., AAA, ICC).  

- **Process:**  
  - Less formal than court but still includes hearings and evidence.  
  - Decision (award) is binding and enforceable under the Federal Arbitration Act.
