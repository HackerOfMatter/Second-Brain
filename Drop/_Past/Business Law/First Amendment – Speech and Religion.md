# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The First Amendment guarantees freedom of expression, religion, press, assembly, and petition — the foundation of democratic participation.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Freedom of Speech:**  
  - Protects political and symbolic expression (e.g., protests, flag burning).  
  - Exceptions: obscenity, incitement, defamation, true threats.  

- **Freedom of Religion:**  
  - **Establishment Clause:** Government cannot endorse religion.  
  - **Free Exercise Clause:** Government cannot prohibit religious practices.
