# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The General Data Protection Regulation grants individuals the right to have personal data erased under certain conditions — a modern expansion of privacy rights.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Scope:**  
  - Applies to organizations processing EU citizens’ data, even outside the EU.  

- **Right to Be Forgotten:**  
  - Individuals can request data deletion if:  
    - Data is no longer needed.  
    - Consent is withdrawn.  
    - Data was unlawfully processed.  

- **Exceptions:**  
  - Public interest, legal obligations, or freedom of expression may override deletion.
