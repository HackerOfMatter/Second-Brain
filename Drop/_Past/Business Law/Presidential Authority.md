# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The president’s authority derives from the Constitution, congressional delegation, and inherent executive powers.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Constitutional Powers:**  
  - Commander-in-chief of the armed forces.  
  - Chief diplomat and head of state.  
  - Power to pardon, appoint, and veto.  

- **Limitations:**  
  - Subject to checks by Congress and courts.  
  - Cannot unilaterally make laws or appropriate funds.
