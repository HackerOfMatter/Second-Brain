# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** To be defamation, the false statement must be communicated to at least one person other than the plaintiff.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Definition(s):**  
  - “Publication” means intentional or negligent communication of a defamatory statement to a third party.  

- **Principles:**  
  - Every repetition counts as a new publication.  
  - Online sharing can multiply liability (libel by reposting).
