## ⚖️ **Civil Standard (Preponderance of Evidence)**

**Summary:**  
In civil cases, the plaintiff must show their claim is **more likely true than not** (i.e., >50% probability).

**Key Points:**

- Lower standard since only **money or rights** (not liberty) are at stake.
    
- Encourages dispute resolution while maintaining fairness.
    
- Used in contract, tort, and property disputes.
    

**Example:**

- In a negligence case, if it’s more likely than not that the defendant caused the injury, the plaintiff wins.
