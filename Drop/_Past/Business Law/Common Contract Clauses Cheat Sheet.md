# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A concise guide to common clauses found in business and commercial contracts.

# 📄 Key Clauses
| Clause | Purpose |
|--------|----------|
| **Indemnification** | One party agrees to cover losses or damages. |
| **Force Majeure** | Excuses performance due to unforeseen events. |
| **Confidentiality** | Prohibits sharing proprietary information. |
| **Choice of Law** | Determines governing jurisdiction. |
| **Arbitration Clause** | Requires disputes to go to arbitration instead of court. |
| **Merger (Integration) Clause** | States the written contract is the entire agreement. |
| **Severability** | Keeps valid portions enforceable if one part is void. |
| **Assignment** | Controls transfer of contract rights or duties. |
