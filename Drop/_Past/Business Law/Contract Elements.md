## ⚖️ At a Glance
For a contract to be **legally enforceable**, it must include five essential elements: **offer, acceptance, consideration, capacity, and legality**.

## 📚 In Depth
1. **Offer:** A clear proposal by one party (offeror) to another (offeree).
2. **Acceptance:** An unqualified agreement to the offer’s terms.  
3. **Consideration:** Something of value exchanged between parties (money, service, promise).  
4. **Capacity:** Legal ability to enter a contract (age, sound mind).  
5. **Legality:** Purpose must be lawful; illegal agreements are void.

**Example:**  
A contract to sell a car for $10,000 includes all five—offer (car sale), acceptance, payment (consideration), legal adults (capacity), and lawful purpose (legality).
