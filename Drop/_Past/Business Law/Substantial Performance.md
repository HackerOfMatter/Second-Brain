# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A party has substantially performed when most obligations are completed and any defects are minor and can be remedied.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Substantial Performance** — fulfillment of essential contract terms, with only minor or technical deviations.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - The nonbreaching party must still pay, minus damages for defects.
        
    - Focus is on the **intent** and **overall value** of performance.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A builder installs the wrong color tiles but meets all other specs — performance is substantial.
