# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Covenants are promises to perform (or refrain from) specific acts during the life of the contract.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Covenant** — a legally binding promise regarding future conduct.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Can be **affirmative** (do something) or **negative** (not do something).
        
    - Violation → breach → potential damages or injunctions.
        
    - Common in **employment**, **leases**, and **business sales** (e.g., non-compete).
        
- **Example:**
    
    - “Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition.”
        
    - “Seller will not start a competing business for 2 years.”

Covenants are the _living duties_ inside a contract — they keep the agreement in motion long after signing.
