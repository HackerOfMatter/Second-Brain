

**At a Glance:**  
All business organizations balance three variables: **liability**, **taxation**, and **control**.

## ⚖️ Liability Spectrum
| Type | Liability Level | Notes |
|------|------------------|-------|
| [[20-1 Sole Proprietorships]] | Unlimited | Owner = business |
| [[20-5 General Partnerships]] | Unlimited (shared) | Joint and several |
| [[20-6 LLPs]] | Limited (for partners) | Protects from others' negligence |
| [[20-3 LLCs]] | Limited | Members protected |
| [[20-2 Corporations]] | Limited | Shareholders protected |
| [[20-7 PCs]] | Limited (except malpractice) | Professionals still liable |
| [[20-8 Franchises]] | Contract-based | Depends on franchise agreement |

## 💰 Taxation Models
- **Pass-through:** [[20-1 Sole Proprietorships]], [[20-5 General Partnerships]], [[20-6 LLPs]], [[20-3 LLCs]]  
- **Double taxation:** [[20-2 Corporations]] (profits + dividends)  
- **Hybrid:** [[20-7 PCs]] and [[20-4 Social Enterprises]] (depends on election)
