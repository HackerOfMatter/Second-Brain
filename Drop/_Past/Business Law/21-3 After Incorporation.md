# 21-3 After Incorporation 🏛️

**At a Glance:**  
Once formed, the corporation gains legal rights and responsibilities independent of its founders.

---

## Legal Characteristics
- **Separate Legal Personhood:** Can sue, be sued, own property, contract, and pay taxes.  
- **Limited Liability:** Shareholders not personally liable for corporate debts.  
- **Perpetual Existence:** Continues regardless of changes in ownership.  
- **Centralized Management:** Run by directors and officers, not shareholders directly.  

---

## Internal Governance
- **Bylaws:** Internal rules adopted by board.  
- **Board of Directors:** Policy-making body.  
- **Officers:** Handle day-to-day operations.  
- **Shareholders:** Elect directors and approve major changes.

---

## Piercing the Corporate Veil
Occurs when courts disregard the corporate entity and hold shareholders personally liable if:
- Corp. was used to commit fraud,  
- Undercapitalized, or  
- Corporate formalities were ignored.

---

## Example
If owners treat corporate bank accounts as personal, courts may pierce the veil.
