# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** How fully each party fulfills their contractual duties determines whether the contract is considered complete, breached, or partially performed.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Contract Performance** — the act of fulfilling contractual duties as agreed upon by both parties.
        
- **Principles / Rules:**
    
    - Performance must meet the contract’s **express terms** (what is written) and **implied duties** (e.g., good faith).
        
    - The degree of completion determines the type of breach and the remedies available.
        
- **Types:**
    
    - [[Substantial Performance]] — nearly complete performance with minor deviations.
        
    - [[Material Breach]] — major failure defeating the contract’s main purpose.
        
    - [[Minor Breach]] — small defect or delay that doesn’t destroy the value of the contract.
