# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The Nixon wage-price control controversy demonstrated that executive economic powers must be authorized by Congress — the president cannot unilaterally control private markets.

# 🔍 Key Facts
- **Background:**  
  - In 1971, President Nixon imposed nationwide wage and price freezes to combat inflation under the **Economic Stabilization Act of 1970**, a temporary congressional grant of power.  
  - Later legal challenges tested whether such actions exceeded presidential authority.  

- **Principle Established:**  
  - The executive can only act with **delegated congressional authority**.  
  - Broad emergency powers must still comply with **statutory limits** and **constitutional review**.  

# 🔍 Significance
- Reinforced **separation of powers** — even urgent economic policies require legislative foundation.  
- Cited in later debates on executive orders and emergency declarations.
