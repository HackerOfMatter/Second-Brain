# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Employers violate privacy law if they access private social media posts without permission or lawful access.

# 🔍 Key Facts
- **Case:** *Ehling v. Monmouth-Ocean Hosp. Serv. Corp.*, 872 F. Supp. 2d 369 (D.N.J. 2012).  
- **Facts:** A nurse’s private Facebook post criticizing paramedics was shown to her employer by a coworker with authorized access.  
- **Holding:** The Stored Communications Act protects nonpublic social media content; unauthorized access constitutes invasion of privacy.  

# 🔍 Significance
- Reinforces that online privacy still applies when access requires consent.  
- Employers must respect digital boundaries.
