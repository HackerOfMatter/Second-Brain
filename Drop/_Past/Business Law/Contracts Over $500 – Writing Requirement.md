# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** UCC §2-201 requires contracts for the sale of goods priced at $500 or more to be in writing to be enforceable.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **UCC Writing Requirement** — prevents fraud in large transactions by requiring written evidence of agreement.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Must include:
        
        1. Quantity term.
            
        2. Indication that a contract exists.
            
        3. Signature of the party to be charged.
            
    - **Exceptions:**
        
        - Specially manufactured goods.
            
        - Admissions in court.
            
        - Partial performance.
            
        - Merchant’s confirmation rule.
