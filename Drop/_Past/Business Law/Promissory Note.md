# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A promissory note is a written, signed promise to pay a specific sum to a specified person at a definite time.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Promissory Note** — two-party instrument: _maker_ (promisor) and _payee_ (recipient).
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Must be unconditional and state a definite amount.
        
    - Used in loans and credit transactions.
        
    - Can be negotiable or non-negotiable depending on wording.
