# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Tort law governs civil wrongs where one party’s misconduct causes harm to another, allowing recovery of damages.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Tort** — a civil wrong (other than breach of contract) that results in harm or injury, giving rise to liability.
        
- **Purposes:**
    
    - Compensate victims.
        
    - Deter harmful conduct.
        
    - Promote social responsibility.
        
- **Categories:**
    
    - [[Negligence]] → unintentional harm.
        
    - [[Strict Liability]] → liability without fault.
        
    - [[Intentional Torts]] → deliberate harm (e.g., [[Assault and Battery]], [[Defamation]], [[Privacy Torts]]).
