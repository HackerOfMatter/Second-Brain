# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The Supremacy Clause establishes that the U.S. Constitution and federal laws take precedence over conflicting state laws.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Source:** Article VI, Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution.  
- **Principle:**  
  - Federal law is the “supreme law of the land.”  
  - State judges are bound by it, even if state laws conflict.  
- **Effects:**  
  - Prevents states from undermining national policy.  
  - Supports uniform interpretation of federal law.
