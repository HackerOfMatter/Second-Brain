# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Attachment makes a security interest enforceable; perfection makes it effective against third parties.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Attachment** — the moment a security interest becomes enforceable between creditor and debtor.
        
    - **Perfection** — legal step that makes the interest public and protects against competing claims.
        
- **Requirements for Attachment:**
    
    1. Security agreement.
        
    2. Creditor gives value.
        
    3. Debtor has rights in collateral.
        
- **Methods of Perfection:**
    
    - Filing a financing statement (UCC-1).
        
    - Taking possession or control of collateral.
        
    - Automatic (e.g., PMSI in consumer goods).
