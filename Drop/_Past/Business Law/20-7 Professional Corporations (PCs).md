# 20-7 Professional Corporations (PCs) 🧑‍⚖️

**At a Glance:**  
Corporation for licensed professionals (lawyers, doctors, accountants).

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** State filing required; owners must be licensed.  
- **Liability:** Limited for business debts, but personal malpractice still liable.  
- **Taxation:** Can elect pass-through or corporate taxation.  
- **Control:** Managed by professional shareholders.

## Advantages
- Liability shield for business obligations  
- Professional credibility  

## Disadvantages
- Personal malpractice not shielded  
- State restrictions
