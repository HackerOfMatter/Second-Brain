# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A compact summary of defenses available in tort law — showing when defendants can limit or avoid liability.

# ⚔️ Common Tort Defenses
| Defense | Applies To | Key Concept | Example |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Assumption of Risk** | Negligence | Plaintiff voluntarily accepted known danger. | Spectator injured at a baseball game. |
| **Contributory Negligence** | Negligence | Plaintiff’s own fault completely bars recovery (few states). | Pedestrian texting while crossing illegally. |
| **Comparative Negligence** | Negligence | Plaintiff’s damages reduced by their fault percentage. | 20% fault = 20% reduction in recovery. |
| **Consent** | Intentional Torts | Plaintiff agreed to conduct that caused harm. | Injuries in a boxing match. |
| **Self-Defense** | Assault/Battery | Reasonable force to prevent imminent harm. | Using minimal force to repel attack. |
| **Necessity** | Property Torts | Act justified to avoid greater harm. | Trespassing to escape a dangerous animal. |
| **Statute of Limitations** | All torts | Case barred after legal deadline expires. | Filing negligence suit after time limit passes. |
