# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Each party’s promise or obligation provides the basis for the other’s — this mutual exchange forms the foundation of enforceable contracts.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Mutual Obligation** — both parties are bound by enforceable duties under the contract.
        
    - **Reciprocal Promise** — one party’s promise is made in exchange for the other’s.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - No mutuality = no enforceable contract.
        
    - A contract fails if one side retains discretion without commitment (“illusory promise”).
        
- **Example:**
    
    - “I’ll sell you my car if I feel like it” → not mutual.
        
    - “I’ll sell you my car for $5,000 if you agree to pay by Friday” → enforceable.
