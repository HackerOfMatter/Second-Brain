## ⚖️ At a Glance
Acceptance is the **offeree’s unqualified agreement** to the offer’s exact terms.

## 📚 In Depth
- Must **mirror the offer** (Mirror Image Rule).  
- Effective upon **dispatch** (Mailbox Rule) unless otherwise stated.  
- If a counteroffer is made, the original offer is terminated.

**Example:**  
Emailing “I accept your offer to buy the car for $10,000” forms a contract the moment it’s sent.
