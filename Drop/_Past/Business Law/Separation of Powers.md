## ⚖️ **Separation of Powers**

**Summary:**  
A constitutional principle dividing governmental authority among three branches to prevent concentration of power.

**Purpose:**

- Ensures **independent functioning** of each branch.
    
- Prevents authoritarian control.
    
- Encourages [[Checks and Balances]].
    

**Example:**

- The President can veto, but Congress can override with a two-thirds majority.
