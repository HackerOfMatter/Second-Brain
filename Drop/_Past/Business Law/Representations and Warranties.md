# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Representations are factual statements made to induce a contract; warranties are promises that certain facts are true and will remain true.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Representation** — an assertion of fact made before or during contract formation.
        
    - **Warranty** — a continuing promise that a fact is or will be true.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - A false representation → **misrepresentation** or **fraud**.
        
    - A breached warranty → gives rise to **damages**, but not rescission.
        
    - Often combined in business contracts as “**Reps & Warranties**.”
        
- **Example:**
    
    - Seller says, “The machine has 1,000 hours of use” (representation).
        
    - Seller guarantees “machine will function for 2 years” (warranty).
