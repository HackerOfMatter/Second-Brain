# **Liquidity Ratios**

Measure a company’s ability to meet **short-term obligations**.

| Name                    | Use                                                                                | Formula                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current Ratio           | Measures ability to pay short-term liabilities with short-term assets              | Current Ratio= Current Liabilities/Current Assets​                  |
| Quick Ratio (Acid-Test) | Measures ability to pay short-term liabilities **without inventory**               | Quick Ratio=Current Assets−Inventory/ Current Liabilities​          |
| Cash Ratio              | Measures ability to pay short-term liabilities using **only cash and equivalents** | <br>Cash ratio = cash + marketable securities / current liabilities |

# **Financial Leverage Ratios**

Measure a company’s use of **debt financing**.

| Name                        | Use                                                | Formula                                          |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Debt-to-Equity              | Shows proportion of financing from debt vs. equity | Debt-to-Equity = Total debt / total equity       |
| Debt Ratio                  | Shows portion of assets financed by debt           | debt reatio - total debt / total eassets         |
| Equity Multiplier           | Measures financial leverage                        | equity multipplier = total assets / total equity |
| Times Interest Earned (TIE) | Measures ability to cover interest                 | TIE = EBIT / Interest expense                    |

# **Profitability Ratios**

Measure a company’s **ability to generate profits**.

|Name|Use|Formula|
|---|---|---|
|Gross Profit Margin|Profit from core operations|GPM=Sales - COGSSales\text{GPM} = \frac{\text{Sales - COGS}}{\text{Sales}}GPM=SalesSales - COGS​|
|Operating Profit Margin|Profit after operating expenses|Operating Margin=EBITSales\text{Operating Margin} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Sales}}Operating Margin=SalesEBIT​|
|Net Profit Margin|Overall profitability|Net Margin=Net IncomeSales\text{Net Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}}Net Margin=SalesNet Income​|
|Return on Assets (ROA)|How efficiently assets generate net income|ROA=Net IncomeTotal Assets\text{ROA} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Assets}}ROA=Total AssetsNet Income​|
|Return on Equity (ROE)|How efficiently equity generates net income|ROE=Net IncomeTotal Equity\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Equity}}ROE=Total EquityNet Income​|
|Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)|Profitability vs. total capital|ROIC=EBIT(1-Tax Rate)Debt + Equity\text{ROIC} = \frac{\text{EBIT(1-Tax Rate)}}{\text{Debt + Equity}}ROIC=Debt + EquityEBIT(1-Tax Rate)​|

# **Market Ratios**

Used by investors to **assess stock value and performance**.

| Name                       | Use                                       | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Earnings Per Share (EPS)   | Profit per share                          | EPS=Net Income - Preferred DividendsWeighted Average Common Shares\text{EPS} = \frac{\text{Net Income - Preferred Dividends}}{\text{Weighted Average Common Shares}}EPS=Weighted Average Common SharesNet Income - Preferred Dividends​ |
| Price-Earnings Ratio (P/E) | Market price per $1 of earnings           | P/E=Price per ShareEPS\text{P/E} = \frac{\text{Price per Share}}{\text{EPS}}P/E=EPSPrice per Share​                                                                                                                                     |
