**Future Value and Present Value**

- Know definitions of future value, present value, and discounted value.
- Understand compounding and discounting formulas.
- Be able to compute FV and PV with multiple periods.

**Compound vs. Simple Interest**

- Know how compound interest works.
- Recognize differences from simple interest.
- Interpret interest-on-interest.

**EAR vs. APR**

- Know how compounding affects required deposits to reach a target future value.
- Compare simple vs. compound interest in context.
