## 🧭 Executive Summary
- **Core concept:** Capital budgeting is the process of planning and managing a firm’s long-term investments to ensure the most profitable allocation of resources.  
- **Why it matters:** Strategic investment decisions determine a company’s future direction, growth, and value.  
- **Real-world relevance:** Used by companies to decide whether to expand, buy new equipment, or launch new products.  
- **Likely use:** Long-term project evaluation and financial planning.

---

## 🧩 Key Concepts & Terms
| Term | Definition | Example |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Capital Budgeting** | The process of evaluating and selecting long-term investments | Deciding to build a new factory |
| **Screening Decisions** | Determining whether a project meets minimum criteria | ROI ≥ 10% |
| **Preference Decisions** | Ranking acceptable projects by attractiveness | Choose highest NPV/PI |
| **Post-Audit** | Reviewing actual vs. expected results | Check project’s ROI after 2 years |
| **Independent vs. Mutually Exclusive Projects** | Independent can be accepted together; mutually exclusive can’t | Build Plant A *or* Plant B |

---

## 📊 Frameworks, Models, or Equations
| Technique | Core Formula | Purpose | Example |
|------------|---------------|----------|----------|
| **NPV (Net Present Value)** | NPV = Σ(CFt / (1 + r)^t) – Cost | Measures value creation | NPV = +12,000 ✅ |
| **IRR (Internal Rate of Return)** | Discount rate making NPV = 0 | Shows project’s yield | IRR = 14% > 10% req. ✅ |
| **Payback Period** | Time to recover investment | Simplicity, liquidity focus | 3.2 years |
| **PI (Profitability Index)** | PV of inflows / Cost | Relative profitability | 1.3 ✅ |

---

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application
1. **Identify investment opportunities.**  
2. **Estimate cash inflows/outflows.**  
3. **Select evaluation method (NPV, IRR, PI, Payback).**  
4. **Assess qualitative factors** (strategic fit, ethics, risk).  
5. **Approve and implement project.**  
6. **Perform post-audit review.**

> 💡 *Interpretation:*  
> Capital budgeting bridges *strategy* and *finance*, helping allocate capital to maximize shareholder value.

---

## 🔍 Conceptual Deep Dive
- **Goal:** Maximize shareholder wealth through value-creating investments.  
- **Qualitative factors:** Risk, social impact, ethics, and alignment with mission.  
- **Capital rationing:** When funds are limited, managers use ranking tools (e.g., PI).  
- **Related topics:** [[8.5 Profitability Index (PI)]], [[Net Present Value (NPV)]], [[Internal Rate of Return (IRR)]], [[Agency Problem]].

## 📚 Real-World Example / Case Connection
- **Example:** Apple deciding whether to build a new data center or expand cloud services — both viable, but only one fits within the current capital budget.
