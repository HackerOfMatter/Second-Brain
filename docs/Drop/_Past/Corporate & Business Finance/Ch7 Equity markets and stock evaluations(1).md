## 🧭 Executive Summary

- **Core concept:** Learn how to value stocks, understand their characteristics, and recognize how they trade in capital markets.
    
- **Why it matters:** Equity financing is the backbone of corporate growth and personal investing. Correct valuation underpins smart investment and financing decisions.
    
- **Real-world relevance:** Investors, analysts, and CFOs use these models daily to price shares, issue new equity, and interpret market behavior.
    
- **Likely use:** Tests, valuation projects, case studies on IPOs, and investment-strategy assignments.

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application (Class Walkthrough)

> Capture the professor’s valuation logic.

1. **Given / Known:** Forecasted dividends, growth rate, and required return.
    
2. **Formula or Framework:** Apply constant-growth DDM.
    
3. **Substitution:** P0=2.10/(0.10–0.04)P_0 = 2.10 / (0.10 – 0.04)P0​=2.10/(0.10–0.04)
    
4. **Solution:** P_0 = $35.
    
5. **Interpretation:** If intrinsic value > market price, stock is undervalued → Buy.
    

> 💡 _Takeaway:_ “Stock value equals the present value of expected future cash flows (dividends).”

## 🔍 Conceptual Deep Dive

- **Assumptions behind models:**
    
    - Dividends grow at constant rate g < r.
        
    - Risk and required return remain stable.
        
    - Efficient markets reflect public info.
        
- **What changes if X variable shifts:**
    
    - ↑ g → ↑ price. ↑ r → ↓ price.
        
    - Higher β → higher required return via CAPM.
        
- **Common pitfalls:**
    
    - Using DDM for non-dividend-paying firms.
        
    - Mixing nominal and real rates.
        
    - Forgetting to use next-period dividend (D₁).
