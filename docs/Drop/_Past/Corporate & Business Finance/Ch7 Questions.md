# 🧾 Notes Receivable – Danica Company: Accrued Interest Example

## 📅 Scenario Overview
On **Dec 13**, Danica Company accepted a **$9,500**, 45-day, 8% interest-bearing note from Miranda Lee to settle a past-due account.

At **Dec 31**, year-end, Danica accrues interest on the note (but hasn’t received payment yet).

---

## 📌 Key Data

- 📆 Note Date: Dec 13
- 📆 Year-End: Dec 31
- 📆 Maturity: 45 days (ends Jan 27)
- 💰 Principal: $9,500
- 📈 Interest Rate: 8% annual
- 📊 Time elapsed as of Dec 31: 18 days
- 📚 Method: Accrual basis (use 360-day year)



---

## 🧮 Interest Calculations

 ✅ Total Interest (Full 45 days):
math
= 9,500 × 8% × (45 / 360)  
= 9,500 × 0.08 × 0.125  
= $95
