# Public Goods, Common Resources, & Free Riders

#LumenModule11 #PublicGoods #CommonResources

Lumen Module 11 classifies goods along two properties: **Excludability** and **Rivalry**.

### 📐 Classification Table

| | Excludable | Non-Excludable |
|---|---|---|
| **Rivalrous** | **Private Goods** (Food, clothes) | **Common Resources** (Fish in ocean, clean air) |
| **Non-Rivalrous** | **Club Goods** (Cable TV) | **Public Goods** (National defense, streetlights) |

### 🚨 Core Failures
1. **Public Goods & The Free-Rider Problem:** Because public goods are non-excludable, people consume them without paying, leading private markets to under-provide or fail to provide them. Government steps in via tax-funded provision.
2. **Common Resources & Tragedy of the Commons:** Because common resources are rivalrous but non-excludable, rational individuals over-consume and deplete them. Solved via permits, regulations, or private property rights.
