# Poverty Line & The Poverty Trap

#LumenModule13 #Poverty #SafetyNet

### 📏 Defining the Poverty Line
The official income threshold below which a family is considered in poverty. Historically set as 3 times the estimated minimal cash cost of an adequate diet (and updated for inflation).

### 🪤 The Poverty Trap
Occurs when government welfare benefits decrease dollar-for-dollar as low-income individuals earn more private income.
* **Disincentive:** The effective marginal tax rate on working is 100%, destroying the financial incentive to work.
* **Lumen Policy Solutions:** 
  1. **Phase-out Benefits:** Gradually reduce government assistance as income rises rather than cutting it off abruptly.
  2. **Work Requirements:** Tying safety net support (like TANF) to job training or work search.
  3. **Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC):** A tax credit that increases with earnings up to a ceiling, incentivizing labor force participation.
