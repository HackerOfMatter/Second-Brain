# Externalities & Market-Based Solutions

#LumenModule11 #Externalities #MarketFailure

An **externality** is a spillover effect (cost or benefit) that affects a third party not directly involved in the economic transaction.

### 📊 Types of Externalities
1. **Negative Externalities (e.g., Pollution):**
   * $\text{Social Cost} = \text{Private Cost} + \text{External Cost}$.
   * Free market overproduces ($Q_{\text{market}} > Q_{\text{socially optimal}}$).
2. **Positive Externalities (e.g., Technology Spillovers, Vaccines):**
   * $\text{Social Benefit} = \text{Private Benefit} + \text{External Benefit}$.
   * Free market underproduces ($Q_{\text{market}} < Q_{\text{socially optimal}}$).

### 🛠️ Policy Interventions (Lumen Framework)
* **Command-and-Control:** Direct regulation specifying limits or required technologies.
* **Market-Oriented Tools:**
  * **Pollution Taxes (Pigouvian Taxes):** Internalize the external cost by setting tax = external cost.
  * **Cap-and-Trade (Marketable Permits):** Limits total emissions and lets firms buy/sell permits, achieving pollution reduction at lowest cost.
