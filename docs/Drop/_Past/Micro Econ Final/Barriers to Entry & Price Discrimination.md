# Barriers to Entry & Price Discrimination

#LumenModule9 #Monopoly #MarketStructures

### 🚧 Barriers to Entry
Monopolies survive because potential competitors cannot enter the market. Lumen categorizes barriers into two main types:
1. **Legal Barriers:** Patents, copyrights, and government franchises.
2. **Natural Barriers:** 
   * **Economies of scale:** A single firm can supply the entire market at a lower average total cost than two or more firms (Natural Monopoly).
   * **Control of a physical resource:** Direct ownership of a scarce input.

### 🏷️ Price Discrimination
Selling the same good to different consumers at different prices for reasons not associated with costs.
* **Requirements:** Market power, ability to segment consumers by price elasticity, and prevention of resale.
* **Effect:** Perfect price discrimination eliminates consumer surplus and converts it entirely into producer surplus, eliminating deadweight loss ($Q_{\text{monopoly}} = Q_{\text{competitive}}$).
