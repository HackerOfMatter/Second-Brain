## Quiz: Oligopoly

**Q1:** Which of the following is illegal in the United States?
**Answer:** cartels

**Q2:** In 2007, five or six major pharmaceutical companies formed a group in order to control the price of vitamins and adjust their production. Such an arrangement is called a ________.
**Answer:** cartel

**Q3:** Which of the following reasons is why oligopolies are inefficient?
**Answer:** They may lack incentives to provide innovative products and high-quality service.

**Q4:** How can oligopolies potentially benefit society?
**Answer:** They can produce using economies of scale to lower average costs.

**Q5:** In a prisoner's dilemma, assuming the individuals can’t communicate, what type of behavior provides the most benefit for each of them?
**Answer:** confess

**Q6:** Mary and Raj are the only two growers who provide organically grown corn to a local grocery store... What is the best choice for Raj if he is sure that Mary will cooperate?
**Answer:** If Raj is sure Mary will cooperate, he should cheat.

**Q7:** Why do economists use game theory to explain oligopolies?
**Answer:** Oligopolies are complex and varied and game theory allows economists to model different variations of competition and cooperation.

**Q8:** What happen when oligopoly firms engage in cut-throat competition?
**Answer:** It tends to result in zero economic profits

**Q9:** Sometimes oligopolies in the same industry are very different in size. Suppose we have a duopoly where one firm (Firm A) is large and the other firm (Firm B) is small... What is Firm B's dominant strategy? Should it collude or cheat?
**Answer:** Firm B should cheat

**Q10:** Take what you’ve learned from game theory and apply it to the framework of an oligopoly. Which of these strategies can work like a silent form of cooperation?
**Answer:** The firms always match other cartel firms' price cuts, but don’t match price increases.

## Quiz: Income Distribution

**Q1:** Which of the following is a potential source of income inequality?
**Answer:** Wage disparity

**Q2:** If incomes rise for both low-income and high-income workers, but rise less for the high-income workers, ________.
**Answer:** then poverty will fall and inequality will fall.

**Q3:** An extremely high degree of ________, with very high taxes on the rich, would be likely to discourage work and entrepreneurship.
**Answer:** redistribution

**Q4:** The poverty line is determined by ________.
**Answer:** the level of income needed to cover basic needs

**Q5:** Which of these are used to determine the poverty line in the U.S.?
**Answer:** Amount of income to cover basic standard of living.

**Q6:** The effects of a poverty trap can be reduced by ________.
**Answer:** initially decreasing the government payments by smaller amounts

**Q7:** Economist use the ________ method to study income distribution in an economy.
**Answer:** dividing a whole group into fifths, or quintiles

**Q8:** A Lorenz curve graphs the ________ received by everyone up to a certain quintile.
**Answer:** cumulative shares of income

**Q9:** A source of income inequality is ________.
**Answer:** family structure

**Q10:** What would probably occur if government policies to reduce poverty or to encourage economic equality, were taken to extremes?
**Answer:** It would reduce incentives for economic output.

## Quiz: Labor Markets

**Q1:** Where does an earning gap continue to exist in the U.S.?
**Answer:** It exists between both gender and race.

**Q2:** The earnings gap in the U.S. labor market between black and white workers ________.
**Answer:** has diminished since 1960, but a gap still remains

**Q3:** Over the past half of a century in the United States, ethnic diversity and racial diversity is ________.
**Answer:** increasing

**Q4:** If U.S. immigration consists of mainly low-skilled workers, then an increase in immigration ________ the wages of low-skilled workers.
**Answer:** will reduce

**Q5:** If immigration consists of mainly high-skilled workers, then a(n) ________ in immigration will ________ the wages of high-skilled workers.
**Answer:** decrease; increase

**Q6:** The concept of derived demand of labor means that ________.
**Answer:** demand for labor is created from a demand for a good or service

**Q7:** The demand for labor is a derived demand because ________.
**Answer:** it is dependent on the demand for output.

**Q8:** What happens to the value of a firm’s additional output sold when the firm has some market power?
**Answer:** The value of that additional output sold is its marginal revenue.

**Q9:** For firms with market power, they choose the number of workers where the market wage equals the firm’s ________.
**Answer:** marginal revenue product

**Q10:** Employment will be lower in a(n) ________ competitive industry than in a ________ competitive industry.
**Answer:** imperfectly; perfectly

## Quiz: Public Goods and Externalities

**Q1:** Why would businesses choose to reduce pollution?
**Answer:** Because their marginal cost is lower or equal to the marginal benefit of the reduction.

**Q2:** Which of the following are examples of positive externalities?
**Answer:** You are a bird watcher, and your neighbor puts up birdhouses and gardens to attract birds.

**Q3:** Markets are inefficient when ________.
**Answer:** social costs are different from private costs.

**Q4:** Which of the following are examples of economic activities with negative externalities?
**Answer:** A gold mine discharging arsenic into a natural lake that it’s using for a tailings pond.

**Q5:** Externalities exist when ________.
**Answer:** social costs are different from private costs.

**Q6:** When the social benefit of producing a good is higher than the private benefit, then ________.
**Answer:** positive externalities exist.

**Q7:** Externalities represent ________.
**Answer:** the costs or benefits received by third parties from the production activities in a market.

**Q8:** A public good is a good that is ________, and thus is difficult for market producers to sell to individual consumers.
**Answer:** nonexcludable and nonrivalrous

**Q9:** If a community has a public vegetable garden at the city park with a take-as-you-wish policy, the veggies would be considered ________.
**Answer:** common goods.

**Q10:** What will most reasonably happen if a large number of individuals choose to be free riders in a city?
**Answer:** The city may never provide the public good.
