# First Rule of Labor Markets & Derived Demand

#LumenModule12 #LaborMarkets #VMPL

Module 12 details how competitive firms decide how much labor to hire.

### 📜 The First Rule of Labor Markets
> A profit-maximizing firm will hire labor up to the point where the **Marginal Revenue Product of Labor ($\text{MRPL}$)** equals the **Wage ($W$)**.

$$\text{MRPL} = \text{Marginal Product of Labor (MPL)} \times \text{Marginal Revenue (MR)}$$

*(In a perfectly competitive output market where $P = \text{MR}$, $\text{MRPL}$ is also referred to as the **Value of Marginal Product of Labor, $\text{VMPL} = \text{MPL} \times P$**)*.

* Hire if $\text{MRPL} > W$.
* Stop hiring when $\text{MRPL} = W$.

### 🔄 Derived Demand
Labor demand is a **derived demand**—it depends entirely on consumer demand for the good or service that the labor produces.
