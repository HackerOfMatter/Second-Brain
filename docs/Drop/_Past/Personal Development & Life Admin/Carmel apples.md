  

🍏 Recipe 1: 

Granny Smith Apples with Honey Caramel, Coffee Grounds, Dark Chocolate & White Chocolate Drizzle

  

  

  

🧂 Flavor Profile:

  

  

Bold and complex — bitter coffee, deep dark chocolate, sweet caramel, bright tart apple, with a creamy white chocolate finish.

  

  

🍎 Ingredients:

  

  

- 6 Granny Smith apples, cold and dried
- 1 cup honey (raw or wildflower preferred)
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ cup dark roast coffee grounds (very finely ground — espresso grind)
- ½ cup dark chocolate chunks (70–85%)
- ¼ cup white chocolate chips (for drizzle)
- Popsicle sticks or skewers
- Parchment or silicone mat

  

  

  

👨‍🍳 Instructions:

  

  

1. Insert sticks into the apples and chill in the fridge.
2. In a saucepan, combine honey, cream, butter, and salt over medium heat.
3. Stir constantly and bring to a boil. Simmer until it hits 245°F (firm ball stage).
4. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla.
5. Dip each apple in the honey caramel, swirling to coat. Let excess drip off.
6. While caramel is still tacky:  
    

- Press dark chocolate chunks into the surface.
- Lightly sprinkle coffee grounds over the apple (a pinch per apple—use sparingly for flavor, not grit).

8.   
    
9. Let set on parchment or silicone mat.
10. Melt white chocolate in a double boiler or microwave in 10-second bursts. Drizzle over cooled apples using a spoon or piping bag.



  

🍎 Recipe 2: 

Honeycrisp Apples with Honey Caramel, Crushed Heath Bars & Flaky Sea Salt

  

  

  

🧂 Flavor Profile:

  

  

Sweet and salty — buttery toffee and chocolate with juicy Honeycrisp bite, balanced by a pop of sea salt.

  

  

🍎 Ingredients:

  

  

- 6 Honeycrisp apples, cold and dried
- 1 cup honey
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- ¾ cup crushed Heath bars (toffee + chocolate)
- Flaky sea salt (Maldon or similar)
- Popsicle sticks or skewers
- Parchment or silicone mat

  

  

  

👨‍🍳 Instructions:

  

  

1. Insert sticks into apples and chill.
2. In a saucepan, combine honey, cream, butter, and salt. Stir and bring to boil.
3. Simmer until caramel reaches 245°F.
4. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla.
5. Dip apples and let excess caramel drip off.
6. Let set for 1 minute, then press in crushed Heath bars by hand or roll gently.
7. Sprinkle a small pinch of flaky sea salt over the top.
8. Cool fully on parchment.
