# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Risk of acceptance or aversion
> How much you embrace risk

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Definition(s):**  
  - **Term**  — definition  
  
- **Principles / Laws / Rules:**  
  - Risk acceptanc
  - degree of certainty regarding the situation
	  - How confident you are in the disision
- **Equations or Frameworks (if any):**  
  - Formula:  
  1. **Define the problem**
	  1. ==Find the issue== needs to be address
	  2. elephant in the room
  2. **Identify limiting Factors**
	  1. ==What constraints exist==
		  1. Money
		  2. Time
		  3. Skills/Expertise
  3. **Develop potential alternatives**
	  1. Given Constraints- what ==options should the manager consider==
  4. **Analyze the alternative**
	  1. Evaluate the identified options using at least 3 criteria
		  1. Feasiability - can this be done
		  2. Effectiveness - extent to which the option will solve the problem
		  3. Consequences - financial and non financial cost of the organization
  5. **Select the best alternative**
	  1. Based on the options evaluations, the manager decides what course to take
  6. **Implement the decision**
	  1. Executing the selected course of action
  7. **Establish a control and and evaluation system**
	  1. When the manage determines how the decision will be monitored and accessed
