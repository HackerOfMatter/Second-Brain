# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Certain activities are so inherently dangerous that anyone engaging in them is liable for any resulting harm, no matter the precautions taken.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Examples:**  
  - Blasting with dynamite  
  - Keeping wild animals  
  - Transporting toxic or radioactive materials  

- **Principles:**  
  - Risk cannot be eliminated through reasonable care.  
  - The law imposes **automatic liability** for damages caused.  
  - Rooted in public policy — those who create extreme risks bear the cost of resulting harm.

# 🪞 Personal Note
In ultrahazardous work, *safety doesn’t erase risk* — the law treats danger itself as blameworthy.
