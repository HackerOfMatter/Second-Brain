# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Justice Brandeis introduced the idea that privacy is “the right to be let alone” — a cornerstone of modern American privacy law.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Source:**  
  - Samuel Warren & Louis Brandeis, *The Right to Privacy*, Harvard Law Review (1890).  

- **Principles:**  
  - Individuals have a fundamental right to control information about themselves.  
  - Privacy protects autonomy and dignity, not just secrecy.  
  - Anticipated challenges from advancing technology and mass media.  

- **Legacy:**  
  - Forms the philosophical basis for modern cases involving data protection, surveillance, and digital rights.
