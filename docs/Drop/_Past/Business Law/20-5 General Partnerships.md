# 20-5 General Partnerships 🤝

**At a Glance:**  
Two or more owners share management, profits, and unlimited personal liability.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Agreement (written or oral).  
- **Liability:** Joint and several (each partner fully liable).  
- **Taxation:** Pass-through.  
- **Control:** Shared equally unless stated otherwise.  
- **Continuity:** Ends when partner leaves unless stated otherwise.

## Advantages
- Easy to form  
- Shared responsibility and skills  

## Disadvantages
- Unlimited liability for all partners  
- Conflict potential
