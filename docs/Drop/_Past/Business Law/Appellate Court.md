# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Appellate courts review lower court decisions for legal errors — they don’t retry cases or hear new evidence.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Function:**  
  - Examine records and arguments to ensure correct legal procedure.  
  - Can affirm, reverse, or remand decisions.  
- **Structure:**  
  - Intermediate appellate courts (state or federal).  
  - Highest appellate court → Supreme Court.

Appellate courts are **guardians of precedent** — they fix errors, not facts.
