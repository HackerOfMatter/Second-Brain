# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Occurs when a third party intentionally and improperly disrupts another’s contractual or business relationship, causing financial harm.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Elements:**  
  1. Existence of a valid contract or economic expectancy.  
  2. Knowledge of that relationship by the interferer.  
  3. Intentional and unjustified interference.  
  4. Damage resulting from the disruption.  

- **Examples:**  
  - Inducing breach of contract.  
  - Interfering with a merger or supplier relationship.  

- **Defenses:**  
  - Legitimate competition.  
  - Protection of one’s own financial interest.
