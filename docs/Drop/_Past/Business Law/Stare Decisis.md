# Stare Decisis

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
**Stare decisis** means “to stand by things decided.” Courts follow precedents from previous rulings to ensure consistency.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
- Ensures **predictability, fairness, and stability** in the law.
- Judges are generally bound by prior appellate decisions in the same jurisdiction.
- **When can it be overturned?**
  - If the precedent is outdated, unjust, or conflicts with higher law.
- **Case Example:** Brown v. Board of Education overturned *Plessy v. Ferguson*.
