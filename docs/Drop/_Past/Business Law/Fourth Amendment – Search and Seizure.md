# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Protects individuals from unreasonable government searches and seizures, requiring warrants supported by probable cause.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Key Principles:**  
  - Warrants must describe place and items with specificity.  
  - Warrantless searches allowed in limited exceptions (e.g., consent, exigent circumstances).  
- **Exclusionary Rule:**  
  - Illegally obtained evidence cannot be used in court (*Mapp v. Ohio*).
