# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Determines whether a mixed contract (goods + services) is governed by UCC Article 2 or common law based on which aspect predominates.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Predominant Purpose Test** — legal test to decide whether a contract’s primary focus is the sale of goods or the provision of services.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - If **goods dominate** → apply **UCC Article 2**.
        
    - If **services dominate** → apply **common law**.
        
    - Courts consider:
        
        1. Contract’s language.
            
        2. Nature of business of supplier.
            
        3. Intrinsic worth of goods vs services.
            
- **Example:**
    
    - Buying a custom-designed computer system (hardware + installation). If hardware is main value → UCC governs.
