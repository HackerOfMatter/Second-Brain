# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Article 9 governs transactions that create a **security interest** in personal property as collateral for a debt or obligation.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Secured Transaction** — a loan or credit agreement where the borrower provides **collateral** to secure repayment.
        
    - **Security Interest** — a legal claim or lien on the collateral.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Article 9 covers personal property (not real estate).
        
    - Protects lenders by defining how to **attach**, **perfect**, and **prioritize** security interests.
        
    - If borrower defaults, creditor may repossess or sell collateral.
