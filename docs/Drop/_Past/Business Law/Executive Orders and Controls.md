# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Executive orders are presidential directives that manage operations of the federal government — valid unless they exceed constitutional or statutory authority.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Nature:**  
  - Derive power from the Constitution or laws enacted by Congress.  
  - Cannot create new law or appropriate funds.  
  - Subject to judicial review.  

- **Checks:**  
  - Congress may override via legislation.  
  - Courts can invalidate unconstitutional orders.
