## 🔑 Article III, Section 1: Judicial Power

- **Power**: The Supreme Court has the ==final say== on matters of **federal law** and the **Constitution**
- **Key Terms**:
    - Final Say: The ultimate authority to make a decision or ruling.
    - Federal Law: Laws passed by Congress that govern the entire country.

---

## 🔒 Article III, Section 2: Judicial Power

- **Power**: The Supreme Court has the power to ==hear cases== involving **federal laws** and the **Constitution**
- **Key Terms**:
    - Original Jurisdiction: The power to hear cases in the first instance.
    - Appellate Jurisdiction: The power to review decisions made by lower courts.

---

## 🔓 Article III, Section 3: Judicial Power

- **Power**: The Supreme Court has the power to issue writs of ==mandamus== and ==habeas corpus==
- **Key Terms**:
    - Writ of Mandamus: A court order directing a government official to **perform an act.**
    - Habeas Corpus: A court order requiring the government to **produce** someone in custody and **explain** the reason for their detention.

---

## 🔒 Article III, Section 4: Judicial Power

- **Power**: The Supreme Court has the power to ==regulate the jurisdiction== of **lower** courts and federal magistrates
- **Key Terms**:
    - Jurisdiction: The authority to hear cases or make decisions.
    - Federal Magistrates: Judges who serve in the federal court system.

---

## 🔓 Article III, Section 5: Judicial Power

- **Power**: The Supreme Court has the power to ==establish rules== for its own proceedings and those of lower courts
- **Key Terms**:
    - Rules of Procedure: Guidelines for how cases should be conducted.
    - Rules of Evidence: Guidelines for what evidence can be presented in court.
