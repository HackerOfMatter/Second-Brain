	Diversity Jurisdiction
1. **The plaintiff is a citizen of one state**: The person or entity bringing the lawsuit (the plaintiff) must be a citizen of one state.
2. **The defendant is a citizen of another state**: The person or entity being sued (the defendant) must be a citizen of another state.
3. **The amount in controversy exceeds $75,000**: The total value of the dispute must exceed $75,000.
