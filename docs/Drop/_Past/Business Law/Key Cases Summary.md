# ⚖️ Major Cases Overview
| Case | Year | Principle Established |
|-------|------|------------------------|
| *Marbury v. Madison* | 1803 | Judicial review established. |
| *Brown v. Board of Education* | 1954 | Segregation unconstitutional under Equal Protection. |
| *Miranda v. Arizona* | 1966 | Miranda rights required for interrogations. |
| *Roe v. Wade* | 1973 | Privacy right extends to reproductive choice. |
| *Clinton v. City of New York* | 1998 | Line-item veto unconstitutional. |
| *Palsgraf v. Long Island Railroad* | 1928 | Defined foreseeability in negligence. |
| *Hadley v. Baxendale* | 1854 | Limited consequential damages in contract breach. |
