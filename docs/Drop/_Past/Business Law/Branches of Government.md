## 🏛️ **Branches of Government**

**Summary:**  
The U.S. Constitution divides power among **three branches** to prevent tyranny and ensure a system of [[Checks and Balances]].

|Branch|Function|Example|
|---|---|---|
|[[Legislative Branch]]|Makes laws|Congress passes statutes|
|[[Executive Branch]]|Enforces laws|President executes and agencies regulate|
|[[Judicial Branch]]|Interprets laws|Supreme Court decisions|
