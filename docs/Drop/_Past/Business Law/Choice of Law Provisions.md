# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A choice-of-law clause determines which jurisdiction’s legal rules will govern disputes or interpretations of the contract.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Choice of Law Provision** — specifies which state or nation’s laws apply to the agreement.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Provides **predictability** and avoids forum shopping.
        
    - Courts generally uphold it unless it violates **public policy** or lacks **substantial relationship** to the parties.
        
    - Often paired with **forum selection** clauses.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - “This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California.”
