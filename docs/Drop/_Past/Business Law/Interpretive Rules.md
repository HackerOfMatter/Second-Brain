## ⚖️ **Interpretive Rules**

**Summary:**  
Interpretive rules are **agency statements** explaining how the agency **interprets and enforces its own regulations**.

**Key Points:**

- Do **not** have the force of law.
    
- Do **not require public notice or comment** under the APA.
    
- Provide guidance to regulated parties and agency staff.
    

**Example:**

- IRS interpretive rule explaining how “dependent” is defined for tax purposes.
    

**Compare:**

- **Legislative Rules** → binding, require notice and comment.
