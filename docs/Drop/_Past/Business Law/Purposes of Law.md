# Purposes of Law

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
The purpose of law is to **maintain order, resolve disputes, and protect liberties** in society.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
1. **Social Order:** Prevent chaos through clear rules.
2. **Justice and Fairness:** Apply the same standards to all.
3. **Protection of Rights:** Secure property and individual freedoms.
4. **Moral and Social Values:** Reflect society’s ethics (e.g., anti-theft laws).
5. **Economic Stability:** Support market transactions and contracts.
