# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Article 3 governs written promises or orders to pay money — instruments that can be transferred freely like cash substitutes.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Negotiable Instrument** — a signed writing that guarantees payment of a specific amount of money, either on demand or at a future time.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Must be: written, signed, unconditional, and payable to order or bearer.
        
    - Transfers create rights of a **holder in due course** (HDC).
        
    - Common types: [[Promissory Note]], [[Check]], [[Certificate of Deposit]].
