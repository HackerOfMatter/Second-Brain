# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** The UCC imposes an overarching duty of good faith — honesty in fact and observance of reasonable commercial standards.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Good Faith (UCC §1-201(b)(20))** — honesty in fact and observance of reasonable commercial standards of fair dealing.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Applies to **all parties** in all UCC transactions.
        
    - **Merchants** must meet a higher standard — conduct consistent with trade customs and fair practice.
        
    - Breach of good faith can invalidate performance or limit remedies.
        
- **Examples:**
    
    - Concealing known defects in goods.
        
    - Manipulating delivery timing to disadvantage the other party.
