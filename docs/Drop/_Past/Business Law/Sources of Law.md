# Sources of Law

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
Law originates from **constitutions, statutes, administrative rules, and judicial decisions.**

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
1. **Constitutional Law**  
   - Supreme law of the land.  
   - Establishes government structure and protects fundamental rights.  
   - Example: U.S. Constitution, state constitutions.

2. **Statutory Law**  
   - Laws enacted by legislative bodies (e.g., Congress, state legislatures).  
   - Example: Civil Rights Act, Uniform Commercial Code (UCC).

3. **Administrative Law**  
   - Created by government agencies to enforce statutes.  
   - Example: EPA regulations, IRS tax rules.

4. **Case Law / Common Law**  
   - Derived from judicial decisions.  
   - Principle of **stare decisis** = courts follow precedent.
