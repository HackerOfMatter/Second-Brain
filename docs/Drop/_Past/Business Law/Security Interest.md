# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A security interest is a creditor’s legal right in a debtor’s property (collateral) to ensure repayment of a loan.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Security Interest (UCC §1-201(b)(35))** — an interest in personal property or fixtures that secures payment or performance of an obligation.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Created through a **security agreement**.
        
    - Becomes enforceable upon **attachment**.
        
    - Priority depends on **perfection** (public filing or possession).
        
- **Example:**
    
    - Car loan → lender has a security interest in the vehicle until paid off.
