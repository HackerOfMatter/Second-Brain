# What Is Law

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
Law is a set of **rules enforced by a governing authority** that regulate behavior in society. It maintains order, resolves disputes, and protects rights.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
- **Definition:** Law consists of enforceable rules governing relationships among individuals and between individuals and society.  
- **Functions of Law:**
  - Maintain social order.
  - Protect individual rights and property.
  - Promote social justice.
  - Provide a basis for resolving conflicts.
- **Sources of Law:**  
  - [[Constitutional Law]]  
  - [[Statutory Law]]  
  - [[Administrative Law]]  
  - [[Case Law / Common Law]]

**Example:** Traffic laws prevent chaos and protect public safety.
