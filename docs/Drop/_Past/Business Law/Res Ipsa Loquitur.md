# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** “The thing speaks for itself” — negligence can be inferred when an accident occurs under circumstances that normally wouldn’t happen without someone’s carelessness.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Requirements:**  
  1. The event ordinarily does not occur without negligence.  
  2. The instrumentality causing harm was under the defendant’s control.  
  3. The plaintiff did not contribute to the harm.  

- **Example:**  
  - A surgical sponge left inside a patient after surgery.  
  - A barrel of flour falls from a warehouse window onto a pedestrian (*Byrne v. Boadle*).

Res ipsa lets the **facts speak louder than proof** — some accidents are self-evident signs of negligence.
