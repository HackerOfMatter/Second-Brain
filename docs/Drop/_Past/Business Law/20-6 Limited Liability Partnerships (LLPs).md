# 20-6 Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) 🧾

**At a Glance:**  
Partnership where some or all partners have limited liability.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Must file registration with state.  
- **Liability:** Partners protected from others’ negligence/misconduct.  
- **Taxation:** Pass-through.  
- **Control:** Partners manage business directly.  
- **Continuity:** Continues with remaining partners.

## Advantages
- Limited liability for partners  
- Pass-through taxation  

## Disadvantages
- More formalities than general partnership  
- Limited in some states
