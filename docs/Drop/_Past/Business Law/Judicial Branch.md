## ⚖️ **Judicial Branch**

**Summary:**  
The judiciary interprets laws and ensures they align with the Constitution. It acts as the final arbiter in disputes.

**Key Powers:**

- Conducts **[[Judicial Review]]** (established by _Marbury v. Madison_, 1803).
    
- Interprets statutes and the Constitution.
    
- Resolves disputes among states and individuals.
