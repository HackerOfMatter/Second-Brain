## 🏛️ **Legislative Branch**

**Summary:**  
The legislative branch (Congress) has the **power to create statutory law** and oversee government spending and policy.

**Key Powers:**

- Drafts and passes legislation.
    
- Approves federal budget and taxation.
    
- Confirms appointments and can impeach officials.
    

**Checks:**

- [[Executive Branch]] may veto laws.
    
- [[Judicial Branch]] may declare laws unconstitutional.
    

**Example:**

- Enacting the Affordable Care Act (2010).
