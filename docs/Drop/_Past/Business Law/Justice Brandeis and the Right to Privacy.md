# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Justice Louis Brandeis defined privacy as “the right to be let alone,” laying the philosophical foundation for modern privacy and data protection laws.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Origin:**  
  - From *The Right to Privacy* (Harvard Law Review, 1890) by Warren & Brandeis.  
  - Written in response to new technologies like photography and mass media.  

- **Principles:**  
  - Privacy protects **dignity, autonomy, and control** over personal information.  
  - Anticipated future issues like surveillance and data misuse.  
  - Influenced rulings such as *Griswold v. Connecticut* (1965) and *Roe v. Wade* (1973).
