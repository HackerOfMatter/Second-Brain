1. **Focus on statutory construction**: Interpretive law focuses on understanding the meaning and intent behind legislative statutes.
2. **Analyzing legislative history**: It involves examining the historical context in which laws were enacted to better understand their intended purpose.
3. **Distinguishing between words and meaning**: Interpretive law seeks to distinguish between the literal meaning of legal language and its intended interpretation.

**Key Takeaways:**

1. Interpretive law is a vital component of Administrative Law, focusing on the interpretation and application of existing laws and regulations.
2. It involves analyzing legislative history, statutory construction, and distinguishing between words and meaning to ensure proper implementation of laws.
3. Examples of interpretive law can be found in regulatory agencies, tax laws, and other areas where government agencies need to clarify the meaning of laws or regulations.
