## **1. Business Entities & Corporate Law (Weeks 8)**

|**Entity**|**Owners**|**Liability**|**Taxation**|**Notes / Examples**|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**S Corp**|Shareholders (≤100, US citizens/residents)|Limited|Pass-through|Small business entrepreneurship, no double tax|
|**C Corp**|Shareholders|Limited|Corporate + Dividend|Good for VC/startups; can issue stock|
|**LLC**|Members|Limited|Pass-through or Corp|Flexible; can include corporate/foreign investors|
|**PC (Prof Corp)**|Licensed professionals|Liability limited to entity except malpractice|Pass-through|e.g., doctors, lawyers|
|**GP (General Partnership)**|Partners|Joint/Several|Pass-through|Partners liable for self-dealing, mismanagement|
|**Sole Proprietorship**|Owner|Unlimited|Personal income|Simple, direct control|

**Key Governance & Fiduciary Rules:**

- **Directors**: Holdover director = stays after losing reelection
    
- **Shareholder approval** required for mergers, fundamental changes
    
- **Duty of Care/Loyalty/Good Faith** for managers/officers
    
- **Self-dealing** = breach; may pierce veil for creditor recovery
    

**Startups / Recommendations:**

- VC funds → **C Corp**
    
- Small partnership without LLC → **GP or PC**

## **2. Labor Law & Employment**

- **NLRA Protections:** Employees can unionize; employer cannot:
    
    - Interfere with union formation
        
    - Reward non-union employees to discourage unionization
        
    - Threaten or coerce union members
        
- **Employer Violations Example:** Granting benefits to only non-union employees → **illegal**.

## **4. Bankruptcy (Chapters 7, 11, 12, 13, Subchapter 5)**

|**Chapter**|**Who**|**Purpose**|**Notes**|
|---|---|---|---|
|7|Individuals/Businesses|Liquidation|Assets sold, debts discharged|
|11|Businesses|Reorganization|Company proposes plan; creditors may propose if company doesn’t|
|12|Farmers|Keep farm, restructure debt|Family farm protection|
|13|Individuals|Repayment plan|Plan must be feasible, in good faith|
|Sub5|Small businesses|Simplified reorg|<$7.5M debts, flexible rules|

**Non-dischargeable debts:** Taxes, student loans, fraud, child support

## **5. Consumer Protection, Credit, & Insurance (Weeks 10)**

|**Topic**|**Rule**|
|---|---|
|**Credit Card Loss**|Liability limited once issuer notified; timely reporting crucial|
|**CARD Act**|Protects against unfair fees, interest hikes, requires disclosure|
|**Life Insurance**|Term = temporary, Whole = permanent + savings (cash value)|
|**Annuity**|Payments received over time from premium|
|**Key Person Insurance**|Protects company if vital employee lost|
|**Liability Insurance**|Protects against property damage or injury (rocket/neighbor)|
|**Auto Insurance**|Collision, comprehensive (fire, theft, vandalism)|
|**FTC Act §5**|Prohibits unfair/deceptive acts|
|**False Advertising**|Cannot claim unproven medical benefits|

## **7. Intellectual Property (IP)**

|**Type**|**Key Rules**|
|---|---|
|**Patent**|Protects inventions; prosecution = application process; synthetic genes patentable if non-natural|
|**Trademark**|Protects brand; priority = registration or first use; infringement = likelihood of confusion|
|**Copyright**|Protects original works; fair use exception for education; distribution without rights = violation|

## **8. Bailments & Property Law (Weeks 12)**

|**Bailment Type**|**Duty of Care**|
|---|---|
|Sole benefit of bailor|Minimal care|
|Sole benefit of bailee|High care|
|Mutual benefit|Ordinary care|

**Property / Lease Rules:**

- Landlord duties: deliver possession, maintain habitability
    
- Tenant duties: pay rent, preserve property
    
- Periodic tenancy = auto-renewal unless notice given
    

**Gifts:** Must intend to transfer + deliver; conditional gifts = e.g., engagement ring

## **10. Quick Issue-Spotting Cheat Sheet**

1. **Business Formation:** Who owns, liability, tax, transfer of interest
    
2. **Union/Labor:** Employer interference → NLRA violation
    
3. **Bankruptcy:** Chapter type, trustee role, non-dischargeable debts
    
4. **Securities:** SEC registration, insider trading
    
5. **Insurance:** Type needed, key person, liability
    
6. **Environmental:** Permits, waste laws, endangered species
    
7. **IP:** Patents, trademarks, copyrights
    
8. **Contracts/Warranties:** Formation, implied vs. express, misrepresentation
    
9. **Landlord/Tenant:** Habitability, lease type, obligations
    
10. **Bailments:** Standard of care by type
