# Functions of Law

## 🧭 Summary (At a Glance)
Law functions to **regulate conduct, protect rights, and ensure justice** through enforceable standards.

---

## 📘 Main Concepts
- **Peacekeeping:** Deterring violence and resolving conflicts.
- **Checking Government Power:** Limiting abuse through constitutional safeguards.
- **Promoting Social Justice:** Anti-discrimination, workers’ rights, etc.
- **Facilitating Planning:** Contracts and property law enable long-term business.
- **Providing a Basis for Change:** Through legislative updates and court rulings.
