# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** The Bill of Rights protects individual liberties and ensures fair treatment through procedural and substantive due process.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Due Process Clauses:**  
  - **Fifth Amendment:** Limits federal government.  
  - **Fourteenth Amendment:** Extends protections to state actions.  
- **Two Types:**  
  - **Procedural:** Fair legal processes (notice, hearing, counsel).  
  - **Substantive:** Protects fundamental rights from government interference.
