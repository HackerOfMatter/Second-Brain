# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Checks and drafts involve three key parties — the **drawer** who writes it, the **drawee** (bank) that pays it, and the **payee** who receives the funds.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Drawer** — the person or entity who orders payment.
        
    - **Drawee** — the bank or financial institution directed to pay.
        
    - **Payee** — the recipient of funds.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Drawer owes funds to payee.
        
    - Drawee is obligated to pay only when sufficient funds exist.
        
    - Indorsements can transfer payee’s rights to another holder.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - In a payroll check:
        
        - Company = Drawer
            
        - Bank = Drawee
            
        - Employee = Payee
