# 20-4 Social Enterprises 🌍

**At a Glance:**  
Businesses that pursue profit while also advancing social or environmental goals.

## Core Concepts
- **Formation:** Must declare dual mission in charter.  
- **Liability:** Similar to corporation or LLC (depends on structure).  
- **Taxation:** Treated according to legal form (corp or LLC).  
- **Control:** Balances profit goals with social mission.  

## Advantages
- Attracts investors who value purpose  
- Legal protection for mission-driven goals  

## Disadvantages
- Difficult to measure success  
- Potential for mission drift
