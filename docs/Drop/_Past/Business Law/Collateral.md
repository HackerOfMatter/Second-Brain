# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Collateral is the property pledged by a borrower that a lender can claim if the borrower defaults.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Collateral** — any property subject to a security interest under Article 9.
        
- **Types:**
    
    - **Tangible:** goods, equipment, inventory.
        
    - **Intangible:** accounts receivable, instruments, chattel paper.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Must be described clearly in the **security agreement**.
        
    - Lender’s rights attach upon agreement, value, and debtor’s rights in collateral.
