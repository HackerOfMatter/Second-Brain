# 21-6 Shareholder Rights 📈

**At a Glance:**  
Shareholders are the corporation’s owners, but their rights are indirect — exercised through votes, suits, and information access.

---

## Basic Rights
1. **Voting Rights**
   - Elect directors; approve mergers, dissolutions, amendments.  
   - Each share = one vote (unless preferred shares).  
   - May vote by **proxy** or in **cumulative voting** systems.

2. **Dividends**
   - Declared at board’s discretion.  
   - No guaranteed right to dividends.

3. **Information & Inspection**
   - Can inspect corporate books for a proper purpose (e.g., check for mismanagement).

4. **Preemptive Rights**
   - Right to buy new shares before outsiders (to prevent dilution).

5. **Derivative Suits**
   - Brought by shareholders *on behalf of the corporation* for harm done to it.  
   - Example: directors breached fiduciary duty.

6. **Direct Suits**
   - Brought by shareholders for harm to *their own* rights (e.g., denied voting).

---

## Minority Shareholder Protections
- **Oppression remedies** in close corporations.  
- **Buyout rights** or **appraisal rights** during mergers.

---

## Exam Traps
- Derivative suits = on behalf of corp.  
- Direct suits = personal rights.  
- Dividends = discretionary, not mandatory.
