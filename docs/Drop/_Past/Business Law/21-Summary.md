# 21-Summary | Concept Cluster 🧠

**At a Glance:**  
Corporations operate as *legal persons* — born through incorporation, managed by fiduciaries, owned by shareholders, and eventually dissolved under law.

---

## 🏗️ Life Cycle of a Corporation
1. **Promoters** → Form the idea (liable for pre-incorp contracts)  
2. **Incorporation** → File articles, create legal entity  
3. **After Incorporation** → Gains personhood, limited liability  
4. **Management** → Directors/officers govern under fiduciary duties  
5. **Shareholders** → Elect, vote, and hold management accountable  
6. **Dissolution** → Voluntary or forced winding up  

---

## ⚖️ Core Legal Themes

| Concept | Definition | Key Note |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Separate Legal Entity** | Corporation has its own legal identity | Enables limited liability |
| **Fiduciary Duties** | Duties of care, loyalty, good faith | Basis for lawsuits (e.g., derivative) |
| **Business Judgment Rule** | Protects directors’ good faith decisions | Encourages risk-taking |
| **Piercing the Veil** | Ignoring entity status for fraud/abuse | Rare and fact-specific |
| **Derivative Suits** | Shareholders sue on corp’s behalf | Remedy for mismanagement |
| **Corporate Dissolution** | Legal death of corporation | Requires winding up |
