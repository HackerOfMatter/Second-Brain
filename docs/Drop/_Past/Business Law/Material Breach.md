# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A material breach is a major failure to perform that defeats the contract’s main purpose and excuses the other party’s performance.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Material Breach** — significant nonperformance that goes to the heart of the agreement.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - The injured party can terminate the contract and sue for damages.
        
    - Determined by:
        
        - The extent of performance completed.
            
        - The benefit received by the nonbreaching party.
            
        - The breacher’s intent or good faith.
