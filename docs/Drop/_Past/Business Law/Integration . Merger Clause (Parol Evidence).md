# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** The integration clause confirms that the written contract represents the entire agreement and excludes prior or outside statements.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Integration (Merger) Clause** — states that the written document is the full and final expression of the parties’ deal.
        
    - **Parol Evidence Rule** — bars use of prior oral/written statements to contradict or add to the contract.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Protects against false claims about side promises.
        
    - Exceptions exist for **fraud**, **ambiguity**, or **subsequent modifications**.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - “This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations.”
