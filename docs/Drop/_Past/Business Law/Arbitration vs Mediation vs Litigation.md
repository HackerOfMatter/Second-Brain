# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Arbitration, mediation, and litigation are the main methods of resolving disputes — differing in cost, formality, and control over outcomes.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Mediation:**  
  - Neutral third party helps parties reach voluntary settlement.  
  - Non-binding and collaborative.  

- **Arbitration:**  
  - Dispute decided by neutral arbitrator or [[Arbitration Panel]].  
  - Binding decision, usually final and faster than court.  

- **Litigation:**  
  - Formal court process before a [[Trial Court of Limited Jurisdiction]] or higher.  
  - Public, rule-bound, and appealable to an [[Appellate Court]].
