**At a Glance:**  
Every contract and professional relationship includes an **implied duty to act honestly, fairly, and in good faith** toward the other party.

**In Depth:**

- **Legal Principle:** Found in contract law and business ethics.
    
- **Meaning:**
    
    - Parties must not act to destroy the other’s rights to receive benefits of the agreement.
        
    - Goes beyond strict legality — focuses on **honesty and fairness**.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - A contractor intentionally delays a project to charge extra fees — violates good faith.
        
- **In Business Context:**
    
    - Encourages transparent negotiation and performance.
        
    - Forms ethical basis for [[Corporate and Personal Ethics]].
