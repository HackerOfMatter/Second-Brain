# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A reciprocal promise is when each party’s commitment is both a **promise** and **consideration** for the other’s promise.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Reciprocal Promise** — mutual exchange of promises forming the basis of a bilateral contract.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Both parties become obligated the moment promises are exchanged.
        
    - Enforceable if each side provides **legal consideration**.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - Buyer promises to pay $10,000; Seller promises to deliver a car — both are bound once promises are exchanged.
