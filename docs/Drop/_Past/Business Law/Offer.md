## ⚖️ At a Glance
An **offer** is a definite proposal that gives another party the power to form a contract by accepting.

## 📚 In Depth
- Must show **intent** to be bound (not just negotiations).  
- Must be **definite and communicated** to the offeree.  
- Can be revoked before acceptance unless consideration was given to keep it open (option contract).  

**Example:** “I’ll sell you my car for $10,000” is an offer; “Would you buy my car for $10,000?” is not.
