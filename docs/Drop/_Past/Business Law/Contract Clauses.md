# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** Contract clauses define, allocate, and limit risk between parties, ensuring clarity on rights, duties, and remedies.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Contract Clause** — a specific section within an agreement that governs a particular aspect of the relationship or performance.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Clauses are _interpretive tools_ that define how the contract operates under various conditions.
        
    - Common categories include:
        
        - **Representations and Warranties** (truth of facts and promises)
            
        - **Covenants** (ongoing obligations)
            
        - **Choice of Law Provisions** (which state’s law applies)
            
        - **Integration Clauses** (final written expression of agreement)
            
        - **Riders/Addenda** (custom or supplementary terms)
