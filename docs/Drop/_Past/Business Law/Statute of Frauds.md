# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** The Statute of Frauds requires certain types of contracts to be in writing to be legally enforceable.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - **Statute of Frauds (SOF)** — a legal rule that prevents fraud and perjury by requiring written evidence for specific contracts.
        
- **Principles:**
    
    - Writing must identify the parties, subject matter, terms, and be **signed** by the party to be charged.
        
    - Oral contracts covered by SOF are **unenforceable**, not void — meaning they can still be voluntarily performed.
        
    - Primary categories under SOF include:
        
        - Suretyship (promises to pay another’s debt)
            
        - Real estate transactions
            
        - Contracts that cannot be performed within one year
            
        - Sale of goods over $500 (UCC)
