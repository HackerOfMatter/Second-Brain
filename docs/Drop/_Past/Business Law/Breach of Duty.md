# 🧠 Core Idea

> ⚡ **In essence:** A breach occurs when someone fails to meet the standard of care required by law.

# 🔍 Key Concepts

- **Definition(s):**
    
    - Violation of a duty owed that a reasonable person would have fulfilled.
        
- **Evidence:**
    
    - Expert testimony.
        
    - Safety standards or laws.
        
    - Conduct under similar circumstances.
