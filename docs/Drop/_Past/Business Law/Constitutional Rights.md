## 🔑 First Amendment: Freedom of Speech and Expression

- **Right**: Protects freedom of speech, press, assembly, petition, and religion.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Freedom of Speech: The right to express one's opinions without fear of government reprisal.
    - Freedom of the Press: The right to publish information without government interference.

---

## 🔒 Second Amendment: Right to Bear Arms

- **Right**: Protects the right to keep and bear arms for a well-regulated militia and for other lawful purposes.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Right to Keep and Bear Arms: The right to own and carry firearms.
    - Well-Regulated Militia: A military force composed of citizens who are trained and organized by the government.

---

## 🔓 Third Amendment: Protection from Unreasonable Searches and Seizures

- **Right**: Protects individuals from unreasonable searches and seizures by the government.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Search and Seizure: The act of searching for or taking possession of someone's property without their consent.
    - Reasonable Search: A search that is justified by a legitimate law enforcement purpose.

---

## 🔒 Fourth Amendment: Protection from Unreasonable Searches and Seizures

- **Right**: Protects individuals from unreasonable searches and seizures by the government.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Search Warrant: A court order authorizing a law enforcement officer to search a specific location.
    - Probable Cause: The reasonable belief that a crime has been committed or is being committed.

---

## 🔓 Fifth Amendment: Protection of Life, Liberty, and Property

- **Right**: Protects individuals from government interference with their life, liberty, and property.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Double Jeopardy: The idea that someone cannot be punished twice for the same offense.
    - Self-Incrimination: The act of confessing to a crime or revealing evidence against oneself.

---

## 🔒 Sixth Amendment: Right to a Speedy Trial

- **Right**: Protects individuals from government interference with their right to a fair trial.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Speedy Trial: A trial that is conducted in a timely manner, without unnecessary delay.
    - Due Process: The process of ensuring that all legal proceedings are fair and just.

---

**Seventh Amendment: Right to Jury Trial**

- **Right**: Protects individuals from government interference with their right to a fair trial by jury.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Jury Trial: A trial in which a group of citizens (the jury) is tasked with determining the facts and reaching a verdict.
    - Due Process: The process of ensuring that all legal proceedings are fair and just.

---

**Eighth Amendment: Protection from Cruel and Unusual Punishment**

- **Right**: Protects individuals from government interference with their right to be free from cruel and unusual punishment.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Cruel and Unusual Punishment: A punishment that is excessive or unreasonable in light of the circumstances.
    - Due Process: The process of ensuring that all legal proceedings are fair and just.

---

**Ninth Amendment: Protection of Individual Rights**

- **Right**: Protects individuals from government interference with their right to privacy, reputation, and personal liberty.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Right to Privacy: The right to be free from government surveillance or intrusion into one's personal life.
    - Reputation: One's good name or character.

---

**Tenth Amendment: Powers Reserved to the States**

- **Right**: Protects states from federal interference with their powers and authority.
- **Key Terms**:
    - Powers Reserved: The powers that are reserved to the states by the Constitution.
    - Federalism: The system of government in which power is divided between the federal government and the states.
