# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Privacy torts safeguard individuals from unjustified intrusion, exposure, or exploitation of their private life or likeness.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Four Core Privacy Torts:**  
  1. **Intrusion upon seclusion** — invading private affairs (e.g., hidden cameras).  
  2. **Public disclosure of private facts** — revealing truthful but highly private info.  
  3. **False light** — presenting someone misleadingly.  
  4. **Appropriation** — using name or image for commercial gain.  

- **Modern Context:**  
  - Expands into digital spaces and social media (e.g., [[Ehling v. Monmouth-Ocean Hosp. Case]]).
