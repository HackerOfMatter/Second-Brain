## ⚖️ At a Glance
A contract must have a **lawful purpose** to be enforceable.

## 📚 In Depth
- Contracts for illegal acts (gambling, fraud) are void.  
- Regulatory violations (e.g., unlicensed contractors) may also make contracts unenforceable.
