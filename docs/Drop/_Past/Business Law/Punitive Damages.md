# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** Punitive damages punish outrageous conduct and deter future wrongdoing — they go beyond compensation.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Requirements:**  
  - Willful, malicious, or reckless disregard for others’ safety or rights.  
  - Not awarded in ordinary negligence.  
  - Must align with due process (*BMW v. Gore* limited excessive punitive awards).  

- **Purpose:**  
  - Deterrence and retribution.
