**Groups:** A group exists almost anywhere two or more people interact or coexist. A group does not have a unified purpose

**Mobs:** Unlike groups, mobs have a unified purpose. Mobs of employees often form with the focused intent to challenge, malign, or even sabotage the established order

**Team:** Teams share a common goal. A team is composed of two or more people who interact regularly and coordinate their work to accomplish a mutual objective
