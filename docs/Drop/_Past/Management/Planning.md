# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Definition(s):**  
- Broadest --> Specific
  - **Strategic Planning**  — Determining the overall game plan for the organiazation  
	  - Overall Goals
	  - Executive team makes
	  - 5+ years
  - **Tactical Planning**  — Identifiing the specific steps that need to be taken in order to obtain the goal
	  - Tactical team- bring to executivs
	  - 1-3
  - **Operational Planning** -- crafting a plan the manager uses for their job responsibilities
	  - Team or project
	  - less 1 year
  - **Contingency** -- Identifing alternative courses of action that can be implemented if and when the original plan proves inadequate because of changing circumstances
	  - Just in case plans
