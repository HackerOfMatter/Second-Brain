## 🪜 Procedures / Steps (memorize order)\

**Types of change**
1. Strategic
	1. Adjustments that are made to the organizations way of achieving its goals
		1. Ex: Targeting a new segment
2. Structural
	1. Change that is made to the organiztions’ reporting/cordinating relationships
		1. Ex: Going from different types of structures
3. Process-Oriented
	1. Change that is made to optimize work flow or increase productivity
	2. Effecient and effective
		1. Ex: Initiating production line
4. People-Centered
	1. People to alter the attitudes, behavior, skills or performance of an organizations’ employees
		1. Ex: Changing benefits for employees
		2. Change the coffee for workers— they dont like it
---
**Challenges to change**
- Extent of planning
	- Degree to which the change is ==anticipated== can impact if maagers can successfully navigate it
- Degree of change
	- The extent  whichthe change is significant can ==impact== the managers sucess in navigating it
- Degree of learning
	- The extent to which organizational members ==adapt== to change can influence the change
- Target of change
	- ==Who needs to change==
	- Level of the organizational hiarchy/functional role that is the subject to the change effort can influence the success of the change
- Organizational structure
	- the degree to which the organizational structure is beaorocratic vs organic can i pact the success of the change
---
**Lewin’s Change Model**
- Unfreeze
	- Who is changing and what will the impact be
	- Unintended consequences
- Change/Movement
	-  Managers intitiate proposed change and solicit support from those who are effected from the change
- ==Refreeze==
	- Managers need to reinforce the change behavior and support the new behavior impacted by the change
