## Consequentialist
- based moral judgment on the outcome of an action
- Ends justify the means
### Utilitarionism
	action is morally right if results greatest ammount of good for the greatest amount of people
- Cant quantify values
### Egoism
	Morally right if it meets the self-interest of the descision maker
- everyone acts in their own self interest
### Hedonism
	Action is morally right is it produces the most pleasure or happiness is the sole purpose
- Pleasure Branch
- Happiness Branch

### Christian Hedonism
- God is most glorified in us when we are most satisfied in Him
- greatest joy is found in God, and in finding that joy, we make God's value evident to the world
- means that by deeply enjoying God, especially in challenging circumstances, we demonstrate His worth and honor Him
