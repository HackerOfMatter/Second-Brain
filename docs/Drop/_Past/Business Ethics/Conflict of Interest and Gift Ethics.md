# 🧠 Core Idea
> ⚡ **In essence:** A conflict of interest occurs when personal interests interfere with professional duties — especially through gifts, favors, or biased decisions.

# 🔍 Key Concepts
- **Conflict of Interest:**  
  - When an employee’s judgment is influenced by personal gain rather than company welfare.  
  - Must be disclosed to prevent ethical breaches.  

- **Gift Ethics:**  
  - Small tokens may be acceptable, but gifts that influence decision-making breach corporate ethics codes.  
  - Often regulated by compliance programs or law (e.g., FCPA).
