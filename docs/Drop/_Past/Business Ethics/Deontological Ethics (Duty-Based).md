**At a Glance:**  
Focuses on **moral duties and rules** — actions are right if they follow a principle or duty, even if the outcome is bad.

**In Depth:**

- **Philosopher:** Immanuel Kant.
    
- **Core Idea:** “Do what is right because it is right.”
    
- **Example:**
    
    - A lawyer refuses to lie in court, even if it would help an innocent client — because lying violates moral duty.
        
- **Strengths:**
    
    - Clear moral boundaries.
        
    - Upholds individual rights and justice.
        
- **Weaknesses:**
    
    - Can lead to rigid results (e.g., telling the truth even if it harms someone).
        
- **Application in Business:**
    
    - Duty to disclose truthful information.
        
    - Duty to act with [[Good Faith and Fair Dealing]].
