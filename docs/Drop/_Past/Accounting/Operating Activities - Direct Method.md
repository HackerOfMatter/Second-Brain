### 💡 Concept Overview

Under the **Direct Method**, we reconstruct the income statement on a cash basis.  
You **ignore net income** and instead **convert each revenue and expense item** from accrual to cash.

---

---

### 🧠 Key Takeaways:

- Direct method gives **clear visibility** into cash inflows and outflows.
    
- Net cash is **identical** to indirect method — but calculated differently.
    
- Non-cash expenses and gains/losses are ignored completely.
