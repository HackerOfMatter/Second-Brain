Term: Cognitive Load Theory
Definition: A theoretical framework that explains how our brains process
information and manage cognitive tasks.
Key Concepts:
        * Working memory
        * Attention
        * Processing capacity
Example: Imagine you're trying to learn a new language. As you listen to a
conversation, your brain is processing the sounds, syntax, and vocabulary
simultaneously. This example illustrates the concept of cognitive load.
Connection to Other Terms: Cognitive Load Theory relates to Constructivist
Learning Theory (the idea that knowledge is constructed by the learner) because
it highlights how our brains actively work together to process information.
