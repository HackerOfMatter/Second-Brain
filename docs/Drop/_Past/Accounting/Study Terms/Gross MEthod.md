🟦 Gross Method (Most Common)  
────────────────────────

- You record the full (gross) invoice amount at the time of purchase or sale.
    
- If a discount is taken later, you record it when it’s actually used.
    

🧾 Example:  
You buy $1,000 worth of inventory with terms 2/10, net 30.

- Day 1: Record $1,000
    
- If you pay within 10 days → record a $20 discount later
    
- If not → pay full $1,000
    

👍 Easy to use  
👎 Discounts not guaranteed, so may miss tracking cost savings
