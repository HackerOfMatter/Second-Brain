# 3×5 Flash Cards (front/back)

**Card 1 — FRONT: “The 6 Moves”**

1. Buy materials → **Raw Materials**
    
2. Issue to shop → **WIP (direct)**, **FOH (indirect)**
    
3. Record labor → **WIP (direct)**, **FOH (indirect)**
    
4. Incur other OH → **FOH**
    
5. Apply OH → **WIP** (POHR×driver)
    
6. Complete/Sell → **WIP→FG**, then **FG→COGS** + record **Sales**
    

**Card 1 — BACK: “Accounts Flow”**  
RM → WIP → FG → COGS  
FOH collects actual indirects (debits) and is **credited** by applied OH.

---

**Card 2 — FRONT: “POHR & Applied OH”**  
POHR = Budgeted OH / Budgeted Driver  
Applied OH (job) = POHR × Actual Driver

**Card 2 — BACK: “Drivers & Examples”**  
Drivers = MH, DLH, DL$ (pick one).  
Examples of OH: indirect materials/labor, factory utilities, factory rent/ins., **factory depreciation**.

---

**Card 3 — FRONT: “Production J/Es”**

- Buy mats: **Dr RM / Cr A/P**
    
- Requisition: **Dr WIP (DM), Dr FOH (IM) / Cr RM**
    
- Labor: **Dr WIP (DL), Dr FOH (IL) / Cr Wages Pay.**
    
- OH incurred: **Dr FOH / Cr A/P (or Accum Dep)**
    
- OH applied: **Dr WIP / Cr FOH**
    

**Card 3 — BACK: “Finish & Sell J/Es”**

- Complete jobs: **Dr FG / Cr WIP**
    
- Record sales: **Dr A/R or Cash / Cr Sales**
    
- Record cost: **Dr COGS / Cr FG**
    

---

**Card 4 — FRONT: “Sanity Checks”**

- **WIP ending** = sum of unfinished job costs
    
- **FG ending** = sum of completed, unsold jobs
    
- **FOH**: Actual − Applied → under/over?
    

**Card 4 — BACK: “Common Pitfalls”**

- Don’t put **indirects** in WIP.
    
- Always **apply** OH (even if actual ≠ applied).
    
- Move **only completed** jobs to FG.
    
- When selling: record **revenue AND co**
