Frequent positive feedback has a secondary benefit related to client motivation for their goals. It relates to the Self-determination Theory that posits that our clients are more likely to reach their goals when their needs for confidence, competence, connection and autonomy are fulfilled (Deci and Ryan 1985). Frequent positive feedback aids in developing a client’s sense of control and confidence, competence and autonomy, but it will also improve your connection with them, in essence addressing the three key ingredients to goal achievement as stated by self-determination theory.
